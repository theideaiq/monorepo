# The IDEA — Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 (production) · 24 October 2026 · Owner: Founder & CEO

The live, browsable version with tokens and component previews is **The IDEA Design System** artifact. Engineers: the implementation spec is `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` in this folder.

---

The IDEA is Iraq's everyday-life brand for the curious: one membership, The IDEA Pass, that opens shopping, renting, learning and community. It behaves like a clever, warm Baghdadi friend who knows where to get everything. It owns one colour pair (**The IDEA Pink** with the **yellow bulb**), one mark (the bulb), one line (**اعلگها / Switch on.**) and one promise: the price, the date and the condition you were told, every time.

Use these rules for everything that carries the name: theideaiq.com and its apps, The IDEA Business (business.theideaiq.com), social, Telegram, packaging, the Plus mailer, signage, decks and documents.

## 2. Brand architecture

A branded house: always **The IDEA** + one plain word, for products and community channels alike. The masterbrand pink and yellow bulb appear on every line; each line adds one secondary accent inside the product.

| Name | What it is | Accent | Line it speaks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The IDEA | Masterbrand | The IDEA Pink + Bulb Yellow | اعلگها / Switch on. |
| The IDEA Store | Gaming, tech, books and lifestyle, delivered across Iraq (`/`, `/store`) | The IDEA Pink | Everything worth having, at your door. |
| The IDEA Plus | Monthly membership to rent games, books and films (`/plus`) | Plus Violet | Play it. Read it. Swap it. |
| The IDEA Club | Posts, events, guides and the Telegram community rooms (`/club`) | Club Orange | Find your people. |
| The IDEA Pass | A person's membership: sign-in, the card, orders, Bulbs (`/pass`) | The IDEA Pink + Bulb Yellow | — |
| The IDEA Business | Procurement, hiring and team Plus for companies (`/business`; the dashboard at business.theideaiq.com) | Business Cobalt | Your company, fully equipped. |
| The IDEA Droid | The Telegram bot and mascot | The IDEA Pink | — |
| The IDEA Staff | Internal staff group | Bulb Yellow | — |

- Write **The IDEA** with *IDEA* in capitals, every time, in English and Arabic copy (Latin letters inside Arabic text). Never "Idea", "IDEA" alone, "the idea" or "your The IDEA…" — reword.
- One accent per surface: a Plus page uses violet beside pink and yellow, never orange too. The home page and The IDEA Pass use pink and yellow only.
- Accents are for tags, chips, plan cards and section bands. The primary action is The IDEA Pink on every line, so "the pink button" always means "do the thing".

## 3. Voice and copy

We write like we talk to a friend we respect: short sentences, real words, a little wit, no hype.

**Principles**

1. **Plain first.** Say what it is, what it costs and when it arrives. "Rent PS5 games, books and movies every month. We deliver them, you return them when you're done."
2. **Bright, not loud.** One idea per line. No exclamation marks in the product UI; at most one in a campaign.
3. **On your side.** Talk about what the customer gets, not what we are.
4. **Promise and prove.** Every claim names a number, a date or a place: "delivered to all 19 governorates".
5. **Arabic first.** Social, The IDEA Club and campaigns speak Iraqi Arabic ("اعلگها", "يمنا بـ", "بالسوگ", "اخو الجديد"). Product screens, receipts and legal pages use clear Modern Standard Arabic with Iraqi warmth. English is written as English, not translated.

**Mechanics**

- Sentence case for headings, buttons and navigation: "Join in the app", "See the plans".
- "You" for the customer, "we" for The IDEA, "members" for Plus; never "users" in customer copy.
- Buttons start with a verb: "Rent with The IDEA Plus", "Buy in the app".
- Latin digits with thousands separators, currency after: **25,000 IQD**, **99,750 دينار عراقي**. Dates in Baghdad time.
- Headlines: a plain first line and a highlighted second — "Innovation for / **every aspect of life.**"
- No emoji in the product UI or official copy; sparingly in The IDEA Club and captions, never in place of words.
- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Payment didn't go through. Try another card or ZainCash."
- Never mock competitors; the souq price is a fact, not a joke. No politics or sectarian references; Eid, Ramadan, Christmas, Nowruz and national days are greeted equally.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock unlimited entertainment solutions! | One membership. Endless entertainment. |
| Your order has been processed successfully. | Your order is in. We'll deliver it Sunday. |
| Error 402 | Payment didn't go through. Try another method. |
| السعر الحصري المميز لدينا فقط | يمنا بـ 99,750 دينار عراقي |
| ممنون لاختيارك خدماتنا المتميزة | شكراً! طلبك بالطريق. |

## 4. Logo

**No black in the logo, tiles or brand blocks.** The mark lives in pink, yellow and white only. Black (ink) is for reading text, never for the brand itself.

**The bulb** is the lamp with five rays already used in the app. Never redraw it. **The wordmark** is **The IDEA** in Readex Pro Bold, −1% tracking. **The lock-up** is the bulb, a gap of one-third of its height, then the wordmark; the bulb is 1.6 × the cap height.

| Ground | Bulb | Wordmark | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The IDEA Pink (signature) | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-pink.svg`, `lockup-on-pink-transparent.svg` |
| Paper or white | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-light.svg` |
| Bulb Yellow | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-yellow.svg` |
| Dark mode or dark photo | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-dark.svg` |

- **Avatar:** yellow bulb on a pink circle (`avatar-the-idea.svg`) on every social network.
- **App icon, favicon, Telegram community:** yellow bulb on a pink rounded square, 22% corners (`app-icon.svg`); `app-icon-yellow.svg` (pink bulb on yellow) for seasonal use.
- **Clear space:** one ray length on every side. **Minimum size:** bulb 16px on screen or 6mm in print; lock-up 80px wide.
- **Line lock-ups:** bulb + The IDEA + the line word in weight 600 (the LineMark component). Never a separate logo for a line.
- **Don't:** use black, put the yellow bulb on paper or white (it disappears), recolour the bulb in a line accent, outline it, add effects, stretch it, or set the wordmark in another font.

**Name tiles** carry the avatar language of The IDEA's Telegram community: one bold Readex Pro word on a circle, about 70% of its width.

| Tile | Ground | Word |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The IDEA Club, Droid; The IDEA Plus | The IDEA Pink | White "Club", "Droid", "Plus" |
| The IDEA Staff (internal) | Bulb Yellow | Pink "Staff" |

Instagram highlight rings follow the same rule: a pink circle with one white Arabic word.

**The bulb character** (the smiling bulb with eyes) is the mascot for social, The IDEA Club, stickers, events, empty states and error pages. It never replaces the logo or appears on invoices, legal pages, The IDEA Business material or checkout.

## 5. Colour

Pink leads, yellow lights it up, paper keeps it warm, and each product adds one accent.

| Name | HEX | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The IDEA Pink** | `#D81B60` | Masterbrand: primary buttons, avatars, app icon, the Pink Band, the Pink Mailer |
| Pink Deep | `#B5134E` | Hover and pressed |
| **Bulb Yellow** | `#F4CF08` | The bulb, Highlights, buttons on pink panels, the Staff tile |
| Paper | `#FBF8EF` | Light page ground |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on pink, cards |
| Ink (text only) | `#0F1014` | Body text and headings; never in logos or brand blocks |
| Plus Violet · Business Cobalt · Teal | `#5B3BE8` · `#1F4FD6` · `#0B7A6E` | Accents (white text); Teal has no product of its own |
| Club Orange · Lime | `#FF6A2B` · `#7BC43A` | Accents, fill only (dark text); Lime has no product of its own |

Print: the screen values above are the reference. Ask the printer to match them to Pantone spot colours from a physical proof before any packaging run (Bulb Yellow is close to Pantone 7405 C).

**Readability rules**

- On pink: white text. Yellow on pink only for the bulb and text 24px and larger (3.2:1).
- On yellow: dark text; logos on yellow are pink.
- On paper: dark text; pink text is fine (4.7:1); yellow is never text on paper.
- Errors are a true red (`#B3261E`), always with an alert icon and a word — red and pink sit at a similar lightness, so the icon and word are what tell them apart.
- Every interactive element shows a 2px focus ring.

**Proportions:** product screens are mostly paper (or dark in dark mode), with pink for the main action and brand moments and yellow for the bulb and highlights. Social posts and avatars are mostly pink.

## 6. Typography

**Readex Pro** for Arabic and Latin: one family designed for both scripts, so a bilingual page has one rhythm. Cairo stays as the Arabic fallback. **Geist Mono** for prices and order numbers on screens.

| Style | Size / line | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display XL · Display | 72/76 · 56/62, −2% | 700 |
| Display Arabic | 64 / 84 | 700 |
| H1 · H2 · H3 | 40/48 · 30/38 · 22/30 | 700 · 600 · 600 |
| Body large · Body · Body Arabic | 18/30 · 16/26 · 17/30 | 400 |
| Label · Caption | 14/20 · 12/16 | 600 · 500 |

Never set Arabic below 14px; never letter-space, italicise or fake-bold Arabic; product and model names stay in Latin inside Arabic copy.

## 7. Layout, space and shape

- 4px grid; 1200px max content width; 12 columns on desktop, 4 on phones with 16px gutters.
- RTL is the default: Arabic pages mirror completely; arrows flip; the bulb, numbers, prices and media controls do not.
- Corners: 10px controls, 16px cards, 28px hero panels and plan cards, pills for chips and tiles.
- Depth: borders first, a soft shadow for resting cards, a stronger one only for floating layers. No gradients or glows.

## 8. Signature devices

1. **The bulb kicker.** A small yellow bulb (pink on paper) centred above every post headline, slide title and receipt header.
2. **The Highlight.** One or two headline words on a yellow swipe with dark text; on pink grounds, the words simply turn yellow. One per headline.
3. **The souq price.** First the market price, small and struck through: "بالسوگ بـ 150,000". Then ours, large and bold: "يمنا بـ 99,750 دينار عراقي". Show a market price only if we checked it in the market in the last 7 days and keep the evidence (screenshot or receipt) for 12 months; consumer law can treat comparison prices as advertising claims.
4. **The condition bulbs.** Customers see one of three bulbs, always with the word beside it; letter grades stay internal.

   | Bulb | Arabic | English | Internal grade |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | Full yellow | جديد | New | Sealed |
   | Unlit | اخو الجديد | Like new | A |
   | Half lit | مستعمل | Used | B or C |

   Retired items (grade X) are never shown to customers.
5. **The Pink Band.** One full-width pink band per page with a white headline (one yellow phrase) and a yellow button: the main call to action, store fascia, delivery bags, staff wear.
6. **The Pink Mailer.** Every The IDEA Plus rental travels in a pink mailer with the yellow bulb, the member's name and "العب. اقرأ. بدّل. • Play it. Read it. Swap it."; the packing slip carries the five promises.
7. **The Rays.** Short rounded strokes from the bulb's own rays, fanned or ringed, as a pattern on packaging, backdrops and empty states; yellow on pink. One ray pattern per layout.

## 9. Imagery and illustration

- **Products:** cut-out, front-on or three-quarter, centred; on pink for posts and promos, on paper in the product. Soft even light, no props. Rental and used items show their condition bulb.
- **People:** real Iraqis in real places — Mutanabbi Street, a Karrada café, a campus, a Basra living room. Natural light, candid moments. No stock handshakes.
- **Illustration:** one flat vector style built from the radius scale and the rays — solid shapes, at most three palette colours plus skin tones, no gradients or 3D, no clip-art. Commission a core set of 30 (exams, travel, study, events, Eid, Ramadan, deadlines, jobs, countries) so The IDEA Club never mixes styles.
- **Third-party marks and game art** appear only to describe genuine products.

## 11. Product UI

- Two themes: Paper (light) and Dark, following the system setting.
- The primary button is pink with white text; on pink panels it is yellow with dark text.
- Icons: Lucide, 1.75px stroke, rounded joins, `currentColor`; directional icons flip in RTL.
- Motion: things "switch on" — a fade with scale 0.96 → 1 in 120–240ms, no bounce; reduced motion means opacity only.
- Every text and control meets WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes.

## 12. Places and objects

- **Store fascia and pop-ups:** the Pink Band with the yellow bulb and white wordmark.
- **Shelf price strips:** yellow, with the souq price above ours.
- **Packaging:** pink boxes with a yellow bulb for The IDEA Store; the Pink Mailer for Plus; kraft with a pink bulb for books.
- **Riders and staff:** pink tee or jacket, yellow bulb on the chest, "اعلگها" across the back.
- **Events:** yellow rays on pink backdrops; one product accent per event.
