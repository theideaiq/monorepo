# The IDEA — Brand Guidelines

Version 1.1 · 26 October 2026 · Owner: Founder & CEO

The live, browsable version with tokens and component previews is **The IDEA Design System** artifact. Engineers: the implementation spec is `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` in this folder. The product decisions behind this version (names, menus, prices, policies) are in the **Program Revision v1** document.

---

The IDEA is Iraq's entertainment brand for the curious: games, music, books and films to buy, rent or share, and one Pass that opens all of it. It behaves like a clever, warm Baghdadi friend who knows where to get everything. It owns one colour pair (**The IDEA Pink** with the **yellow bulb**), one mark (the bulb), one character (**The IDEA Droid**), one line (**اعلگها / Switch on.**) and one promise: the price, the date and the condition you were told, every time.

Use these rules for everything that carries the name: theideaiq.com and the apps, The IDEA Business, social, Telegram, packaging, the Pink Mailer, signage, decks and documents.

## 2. Brand architecture

A branded house: **The IDEA** + one plain noun. Five products, one character, nothing else gets a name.

| Name | What it is | Accent | Line it speaks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The IDEA** | The masterbrand | The IDEA Pink + Bulb Yellow | اعلگها / Switch on. |
| **The IDEA Store** | Buy or rent single items; Sell to us | The IDEA Pink | Everything worth having, at your door. |
| **The IDEA Plus** | The rental membership: Plus Glow, Plus Shine, Plus Blaze | Plus Violet | Play it. Read it. Swap it. |
| **The IDEA Club** | The free side: events, news, stories, guides, surveys, and the Telegram group | Club Orange | Find your people. |
| **The IDEA Pass** | Your membership: the card, orders, Bulbs, settings | Bulb Yellow on pink | One Pass for all of it. |
| **The IDEA Business** | Buying, hiring and team Plus for companies | Business Cobalt | Your company, fully equipped. |
| **The IDEA Droid** | Our bot and mascot (section 5) | — | هلو! |

**Naming rules**

- Write **The IDEA** with *IDEA* in capitals, every time, in English and Arabic copy (Latin letters inside Arabic text). Never "Idea", "IDEA" alone, "the idea" or "your The IDEA…" — reword. On Arabic pages the site's own name (logo, page titles) is ايديا; product names stay "The IDEA …".
- **A new name must earn it:** customers join it, pay for it or visit it on its own, and it will exist in two years. Everything else is a plain word: Saved, Cart, Orders, Help, Sell to us, Events, Surveys.
- **Named things inside products:** Bulbs (the points), the Plus plans **Glow · Shine · Blaze**, the Pink Mailer, the Club's rooms. Nothing else.
- **Retired names, never used again:** Megastore, Suite, Spark, The IDEA Post, Discover, Academy, Launcher, Journal, Bulbs tiers.
- **One accent per surface:** a Plus page uses violet beside pink and yellow, never orange too. Home, the Store and the Pass use pink and yellow only. The primary action is The IDEA Pink everywhere, so "the pink button" always means "do the thing".

## 3. Voice and copy

We write like we talk to a friend we respect: short sentences, real words, a little wit, no hype.

**Principles**

1. **Plain first.** Say what it is, what it costs and when it arrives. "Rent PS5 games, books and films every month. We bring them, you swap them when you're done."
2. **Bright, not loud.** One idea per line. No exclamation marks in the product UI; at most one in a campaign.
3. **On your side.** Talk about what the customer gets, not what we are.
4. **Promise and prove.** Every claim names a number, a date or a place: "delivered to all 18 governorates".
5. **Arabic first.** Social, The IDEA Club, the Droid and campaigns speak Iraqi Arabic ("اعلگها", "يمنا بـ", "بالسوگ", "اخو الجديد"). Product screens, receipts and legal pages use clear Modern Standard Arabic with Iraqi warmth. English is written as English, not translated.

**Mechanics**

- Sentence case for headings, buttons and navigation: "See the plans", "Add to cart".
- "You" for the customer, "we" for The IDEA, "members" for Plus; never "users" or "account" in customer copy (a person's membership *is* The IDEA Pass).
- Buttons start with a verb: "Rent with The IDEA Plus", "Buy", "Rent for 7 days", "Sell to us".
- Latin digits with thousands separators, currency after: **22,750 IQD**, **99,750 دينار عراقي**. Store prices end in …4,750 or …9,750. Dates in Baghdad time.
- Plus plans always carry their item count: "Plus Shine · 2 items at a time".
- Bulbs are written as a number and the word, with the value nearby when it matters: "120 Bulbs (12,000 IQD)".
- Headlines: a plain first line and a highlighted second — "One membership. / **Endless entertainment.**"
- No emoji in the product UI or official copy; sparingly in The IDEA Club and captions, never in place of words.
- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Payment didn't go through. Try another card or pay on delivery."
- Never mock competitors; the souq price is a fact, not a joke. No politics or sectarian references; Eid, Ramadan, Christmas, Nowruz and national days are greeted equally.

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Unlock unlimited entertainment solutions! | One membership. Endless entertainment. |
| Your order has been processed successfully. | Your order is in. We'll deliver it Sunday. |
| Error 402 | Payment didn't go through. Try another method. |
| Upgrade your tier to earn more rewards | Join Plus and earn up to 6% back in Bulbs. |
| السعر الحصري المميز لدينا فقط | يمنا بـ 99,750 دينار عراقي |
| ممنون لاختيارك خدماتنا المتميزة | شكراً! طلبك بالطريق. |

## 4. Logo

**No black in the logo, tiles or brand blocks.** The mark lives in pink, yellow and white only. Black (ink) is for reading text, never for the brand itself.

**The bulb** is the lamp with five rays already used in the app. Never redraw it. **The wordmark** is **The IDEA** in Readex Pro Bold, −1% tracking. **The lock-up** is the bulb, a gap of one-third of its height, then the wordmark; the bulb is 1.6 × the cap height.

| Ground | Bulb | Wordmark | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The IDEA Pink (signature) | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-pink.svg`, `lockup-on-pink-transparent.svg` |
| Paper or white | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-light.svg` |
| Bulb Yellow | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-yellow.svg` |
| Dark mode or dark photo | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-dark.svg` |

- **Avatar:** yellow bulb on a pink circle (`avatar-the-idea.svg`) on every social network and the Telegram channel.
- **App icon and favicon:** yellow bulb on a pink rounded square, 22% corners (`app-icon.svg`); `app-icon-yellow.svg` (pink bulb on yellow) for seasonal use.
- **Clear space:** one ray length on every side. **Minimum size:** bulb 16px on screen or 6mm in print; lock-up 80px wide.
- **Line lock-ups:** bulb + The IDEA + the product word in weight 600 (the LineMark component). Never a separate logo for a product.
- **Don't:** use black, put the yellow bulb on paper or white (it disappears), recolour the bulb in a product accent, outline it, add effects, stretch it, or set the wordmark in another font.

**Name tiles** are one bold Readex Pro word on a circle, about 70% of its width, for chats and highlight rings.

| Tile | Ground | Word | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `tile-club.svg` | The IDEA Pink | White "Club" | The IDEA Club group |
| `tile-plus.svg` | The IDEA Pink | White "Plus" | Plus highlights and promos |
| `tile-staff.svg` | Bulb Yellow | Pink "Staff" | The internal staff group |

The Droid uses its own face (`droid-avatar.png`), not a tile. Instagram highlight rings follow the tile rule: a pink circle with one white Arabic word.

## 5. The IDEA Droid

The Droid is our bot given a body: a small, quick, warm Baghdadi robot whose head is the bulb from the logo. He is curious, helpful and a little cheeky; never sarcastic, never rude. He speaks Iraqi Arabic first.

**Built from the logo**

| Part | Look | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head | Bulb Yellow glass with a white highlight | The logo's bulb |
| Rays | The logo's five rays | His mood light |
| Collar | Two Paper-coloured bands | The bulb's screw base |
| Body | The IDEA Pink, with a yellow power light on the chest (it can turn into a heart) | The masterbrand |
| Gloves | White mitts | Read at sticker size; hold props (the Pink Mailer, a controller, a book, an istikan of chai) |
| Lines | Pink Deep `#B5134E` | No black, ever; on stickers, a white cut-line |

**Mood light**

| Rays | When |
| --- | --- |
| On (yellow) | Everyday, done, "تمام" |
| Blaze (longer, seven rays) | Excited, "اعلگها", good morning |
| Dim | Thinking, sleepy |
| Off (grey) | Sad, errors, good night |

**Where he appears:** the Telegram bot and its replies, WhatsApp, "Ask Droid" help on the site, stickers, social posts and Stories, Club events, empty states, onboarding tips, 404 and error pages, merch.

**Where he never appears:** inside the logo or lock-up, checkout and payments, invoices and receipts, legal pages, The IDEA Business material, next to prices or souq-price claims, serious messages (outages, refunds, safety notices).

**Files:** `droid-hero.png`, `droid-avatar.png` (bot profile photo, 640 × 640) and the 24-sticker pack, vol. 1, in the Droid design canvas. Never redraw him freehand: extend the set from the canvas's parts.

## 6. Colour

Pink leads, yellow lights it up, paper keeps it warm, and each product adds one accent.

| Name | HEX | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The IDEA Pink** | `#D81B60` | Masterbrand: primary buttons, avatars, app icon, the Pink Band, the Pink Mailer, the Droid's body |
| Pink Deep | `#B5134E` | Hover and pressed; the Droid's line work |
| **Bulb Yellow** | `#F4CF08` | The bulb, Bulbs, Highlights, buttons on pink panels, the Staff tile, the Droid's head |
| Paper | `#FBF8EF` | Light page ground |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on pink, cards |
| Ink (text only) | `#0F1014` | Body text and headings; never in logos, tiles, the Droid or brand blocks |
| Plus Violet | `#5B3BE8` | The IDEA Plus (white text) |
| Club Orange | `#FF6A2B` | The IDEA Club, fill only (dark text) |
| Business Cobalt | `#1F4FD6` | The IDEA Business and the info colour (white text) |

Print: the screen values above are the reference. Ask the printer to match them to Pantone spot colours from a physical proof before any packaging run (Bulb Yellow is close to Pantone 7405 C).

**Readability rules**

- On pink: white text. Yellow on pink only for the bulb and text 24px and larger (3.2:1).
- On yellow: dark text; logos on yellow are pink.
- On paper: dark text; pink text is fine (4.7:1); yellow is never text on paper.
- Errors are a true red (`#B3261E`), always with an alert icon and a word — red and pink sit at a similar lightness, so the icon and word are what tell them apart.
- Every interactive element shows a 2px focus ring.

**Proportions:** product screens are mostly paper (or dark in dark mode), with pink for the main action and brand moments and yellow for the bulb, Bulbs and highlights. Social posts and avatars are mostly pink.

## 7. Typography

**Readex Pro** for Arabic and Latin: one family designed for both scripts, so a bilingual page has one rhythm. Cairo stays as the Arabic fallback. **Geist Mono** for prices and order numbers on screens.

| Style | Size / line | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display XL · Display | 72/76 · 56/62, −2% | 700 |
| Display Arabic | 64 / 84 | 700 |
| H1 · H2 · H3 | 40/48 · 30/38 · 22/30 | 700 · 600 · 600 |
| Body large · Body · Body Arabic | 18/30 · 16/26 · 17/30 | 400 |
| Label · Caption | 14/20 · 12/16 | 600 · 500 |

Never set Arabic below 14px; never letter-space, italicise or fake-bold Arabic; product and model names stay in Latin inside Arabic copy.

## 8. Layout, space and shape

- 4px grid; 1200px max content width; 12 columns on desktop, 4 on phones with 16px gutters.
- RTL is the default: Arabic pages mirror completely; arrows flip; the bulb, numbers, prices and media controls do not.
- Corners: 10px controls, 16px cards, 28px hero panels and plan cards, pills for chips and tiles.
- Depth: borders first, a soft shadow for resting cards, a stronger one only for floating layers. No gradients or glows.

## 9. Signature devices

1. **The bulb kicker.** A small yellow bulb (pink on paper) centred above every post headline, slide title and receipt header.
2. **The Highlight.** One or two headline words on a yellow swipe with dark text; on pink grounds, the words simply turn yellow. One per headline.
3. **The souq price.** First the market price, small and struck through: "بالسوگ بـ 150,000". Then ours, large and bold: "يمنا بـ 99,750 دينار عراقي". Show a market price only if we checked it in the market in the last 7 days and keep the evidence (screenshot or receipt) for 12 months; consumer law can treat comparison prices as advertising claims.
4. **Bulbs.** Our points: 1 Bulb = 100 IQD. The small yellow bulb icon is reserved for Bulbs and nothing else (not condition, not plans). Everyone earns 3% back; Plus Glow 4%, Shine 5%, Blaze 6%. There are no spend tiers and no tier colours.
5. **Condition labels.** Every copy for sale shows one plain label with a small four-step meter (never a bulb). Letter grades stay internal.

   | Meter | Arabic | English | Means |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 4 of 4 | جديد | New | Sealed, never used |
   | 3 of 4 | اخو الجديد | Open box | Box opened, unused or barely used |
   | 2 of 4 | مجدّد | Refurbished | Tested and repaired, with a written warranty |
   | 1 of 4 | مستعمل | Used | Works as described; signs of use |

   Rentals show no condition label. Retired copies are never shown to customers.
6. **Plus plan marks.** The plan's light name in Plus Violet with its item count: **Plus Glow · 1 item**, **Plus Shine · 2 items**, **Plus Blaze · 4 items**. On the Pass card the plan shows as a badge; the card itself stays pink for everyone.
7. **The Pink Band.** One full-width pink band per page with a white headline (one yellow phrase) and a yellow button: the main call to action, store fascia, delivery bags, staff wear.
8. **The Pink Mailer.** Every Plus rental travels in a pink mailer with the yellow bulb, the member's name and "العب. اقرأ. بدّل. • Play it. Read it. Swap it."; the packing slip carries the five promises.
9. **The Rays.** Short rounded strokes from the bulb's own rays, fanned or ringed, as a pattern on packaging, backdrops and empty states; yellow on pink. One ray pattern per layout.

## 10. Imagery and illustration

- **Products:** cut-out, front-on or three-quarter, centred; on pink for posts and promos, on paper in the product. Soft even light, no props. Items for sale show their condition label.
- **People:** real Iraqis in real places — Mutanabbi Street, a Karrada café, a campus, a Basra living room. Natural light, candid moments. No stock handshakes.
- **Illustration:** one flat vector style built from the radius scale and the rays — solid shapes, at most three palette colours plus skin tones, no gradients or 3D, no clip-art. The Droid is the only character; commission a core set of 30 scenes (exams, travel, study, events, Eid, Ramadan, deadlines, jobs, countries) in his style so the Club never mixes styles.
- **Third-party marks and game art** appear only to describe genuine products.

## 12. Product UI and navigation

- **Phone tabs:** Store · Plus · Club · Pass.
- **Top bar on every screen:** logo · search · Saved (heart) · Cart · the Pass on desktop. Hidden during sign-in and checkout.
- **Desktop header:** Store · Plus · Club, then "For business", Saved, Cart and Pass. **Footer:** Store · Plus · Club · Pass · Business, then About · Careers · Help · Legal.
- **Personal pages:** `/plus` and `/club` become personal when signed in (your rentals and queue; your tickets and surveys). Your Pass has four pages: the card, Orders, Bulbs, Settings.
- Two themes: Paper (light) and Dark, following the system setting.
- The primary button is pink with white text; on pink panels it is yellow with dark text.
- Icons: Lucide, 1.75px stroke, rounded joins, `currentColor`; directional icons flip in RTL. The bulb icon only for Bulbs.
- Motion: things "switch on" — a fade with scale 0.96 → 1 in 120–240ms, no bounce; reduced motion means opacity only.
- Every text and control meets WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. Games show their age rating.
