# The IDEA — Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 · updated 28 October 2026 · Owner: Founder & CEO

The live tokens and components are in The IDEA design system; packaging is in the separate Packaging guide. Engineers: the implementation spec is `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` in this folder.

---

The IDEA is Iraq's entertainment brand for the curious: games, music, books, films and gear to buy, rent, bid on or share, from us and from shops across Iraq, and one Pass that opens all of it. It behaves like a clever, warm Baghdadi friend who knows where to get everything. It owns:

- one color pair: **The IDEA Pink** with the **yellow bulb**;
- one mark: the bulb;
- one line: **اعلگها / Switch on.**;
- one promise: the price, the date and the condition you were told, every time, from every seller.

These rules cover everything that carries the name: theideaiq.com and the apps, Partners, Staff, social, Telegram, packaging, events, decks and documents.

**Naming rules**

- **The masterbrand:** **The IDEA**, with *IDEA* in capitals, every time, in English copy. Never "Idea", "IDEA" alone or "the idea". In Arabic copy it is **ايديا**, everywhere (see "Arabic names" below).
- **Full names in titles and first mentions:** The IDEA Store, The IDEA Plus, The IDEA Club, The IDEA Pass. Use them in titles, in ads, store listings and social bios, and the first time a product is named somewhere The IDEA isn't already the context (a press note, a partner's site, a poster).
- **Short names everywhere The IDEA is already the context:** inside our website and apps, our emails, our documents and our own channels, write Store, Plus, Club and Pass. "Rent with Plus", "Open the Club".
- **Partners, Droid and Staff are always short.** Never "The IDEA Partners", "The IDEA Droid" or "The IDEA Staff".
- **Always capitalized:** Store, Plus, Club, Pass, Partners, Droid, Staff, Bulbs, and the plans Glow, Shine, Blaze and Duo, in English copy. The plans keep their Latin names inside Arabic copy too.
- **Someone's own:** use the short name with a possessive: your Pass, my Plus, his Pass, your Saved list. Never "your The IDEA Pass".
- **The product itself:** when you talk about the product rather than someone's own, use its full name outside our surfaces ("The IDEA Plus lets you rent games…") and "the" + short name inside them ("the Club has three parts").
- **People:** people on Plus are Plus members. Never "account", "membership" or "users" in customer copy.
- **A new name must earn it:** customers join it, pay for it or visit it on its own, and it will exist in two years. Everything else is a plain word: Saved, Lists, Queue, Cart, Orders, Help, Make an offer, Auctions, Get offers, Events, Opportunities, Surveys.
- **Retired names, never used again:** Megastore, Suite, Spark, Business, The IDEA Post, Discover, Academy, Launcher, Journal, The IDEA Space, Sell to us, boxes, the souq price, The IDEA Partners, The IDEA Droid.

## 3. Voice and copy

We write like we talk to a friend we respect: short sentences, real words, a little wit, no hype.

**Principles**

1. **Plain first.** Say what it is, what it costs and when it arrives. "Rent PS5 games, books and films every month. We bring them, and swap them when you're done."
2. **Bright, not loud.** One idea per line. No exclamation marks in the product UI; at most one in a campaign.
3. **On your side.** Talk about what the customer gets, not what we are.
4. **Promise and prove.** Every claim names a number, a date or a place: "delivered to all 19 governorates".
5. **Arabic first.** Social, the Club, Droid and campaigns speak Iraqi Arabic ("اعلگها", "يمنا بـ"). Product screens, receipts and legal pages use clear Modern Standard Arabic with Iraqi warmth. English is written as English, not translated.

**Mechanics**

- Sentence case for headings, buttons and navigation: "See the plans", "Add to cart".
- "You" for the customer, "we" for The IDEA.
- Buttons start with a verb: "Rent with Plus", "Buy", "Rent for 7 days", "Make an offer", "Place bid", "Send as a gift".
- Latin digits with thousands separators, currency after: **22,750 IQD**, **99,750 دينار عراقي**. The IDEA's own prices end in …4,750 or …9,750. Dates in Baghdad time.
- Plus plans carry their item count: "Plus Shine · 2 items at a time".
- **Money and Bulbs:** prices, payments, payouts, fees and deposits are in IQD; rewards and anything The IDEA gives away are in Bulbs (1 Bulb = 1 IQD). Write "Earn 340 Bulbs" and "2,340 Bulbs = 2,340 IQD off"; in Arabic copy "اكسب 340 گلوب" and "2,340 گلوب = خصم 2,340 دينار". Never a rewards percentage on a product; plan cards are the one place a rate appears.
- **Sellers:** a shop's offer always says who sells it: "Sold by <shop> · delivered and paid through The IDEA".
- **Honest prices:** a struck-through "was" price appears only when that offer really sold at that price (15 of the last 30 days). "Lowest in 30 days" backs any "lowest" claim.
- Headlines: a plain first line and a highlighted second: "One Pass. / **Everything worth having.**"
- No emoji in the product UI or official copy; sparingly in the Club and captions, never in place of words.
- Errors say what happened and what to do: "Payment didn't go through. Try another card or pay on delivery."
- Never mock competitors or other shops. No politics or sectarian references; Eid, Ramadan, Christmas, Nowruz and national days are greeted equally.

## 4. Logo

**No black in the logo, tiles or brand blocks.** The mark lives in pink, yellow and white only. Black (ink) is for reading text, never for the brand itself.

**The bulb** is the lamp with five rays already used in the app. Never redraw it. **The wordmark** is **The IDEA** set in Alexandria Bold, −1% tracking. In the website and apps it is live text beside the bulb; in the logo files it is outlined. **The lock-up** is the bulb, a gap of one-third of its height, then the wordmark; the bulb is 1.6 × the cap height.

| Ground | Bulb | Wordmark | File |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The IDEA Pink (signature) | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-pink.svg`, `lockup-on-pink-transparent.svg` |
| Paper or white | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-light.svg` |
| Bulb Yellow | Pink | Pink | `lockup-on-yellow.svg` |
| Dark mode or dark photo | Yellow | White | `lockup-on-dark.svg` |

- **Avatar:** yellow bulb on a pink circle (`avatar-the-idea.svg`) on every social network and the Telegram channel.
- **App icon and favicon:** yellow bulb on a pink rounded square, 22% corners (`app-icon.svg`); `app-icon-yellow.svg` (pink bulb on yellow) for seasonal use.
- **Clear space:** one ray length on every side. **Minimum size:** bulb 16px on screen or 6mm in print; lock-up 80px wide.
- **Product lock-ups:** bulb + The IDEA + the short name in Alexandria 600. Never a separate logo for a product.
- **"Sold on The IDEA":** shops may show this with `lockup-on-light.svg` or `lockup-on-pink.svg` next to their own logo, never merged with it. A shop never uses the bulb as its own mark.
- **Don't:** use black, put the yellow bulb on paper or white (it disappears), recolor the bulb, outline it, add effects, stretch it, or set the wordmark in another font.

**Name tiles** are one bold word on a circle, about 70% of its width, for chats, highlight rings and app shortcuts. All are pink and white except Staff.

## 5. Droid

Droid is our Telegram bot: order updates, Bulbs, FAQs and help, in Iraqi Arabic first. It helps; it never sells.

- **Look:** the Droid name tile (`tile-droid.svg`) is its profile photo. There is no mascot character: the bulb is the brand's only figure, and a new character, if we ever want one, comes as its own project.
- **Voice:** short, warm and helpful, a little cheeky, never sarcastic. It greets with "هلو!" and confirms with "تمام".
- **Where it speaks:** Telegram, and "Ask Droid" in Help on the site.
- **Never:** in checkout, payments, invoices, legal pages or serious messages (outages, refunds, safety notices).

## 6. Color

One palette for everything. Pink leads, yellow lights it up, paper keeps it warm. Products are told apart by their names, never by colors of their own.

| Name | HEX | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The IDEA Pink** | `#D81B60` | Masterbrand: primary buttons, avatars, app icon, name tiles, the Pink Band, the Pink Mailer, packaging |
| Pink Deep | `#B5134E` | Hover and pressed |
| **Bulb Yellow** | `#F4CF08` | The bulb, Bulbs, Highlights, buttons on pink panels, the Staff tile |
| Paper | `#FBF8EF` | Light page ground |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Text on pink, cards |
| Ink (text only) | `#0F1014` | Body text and headings; never in logos, tiles or brand blocks |

**Status colors** are for states in the product only (never for brand, products or packaging), always with an icon and a word: success `#127A4B`, warning `#9A6700`, danger `#B3261E`.

Print: the screen values above are the reference. Match them to Pantone spot colors from a physical proof before any packaging run (Bulb Yellow is close to Pantone 7405 C).

**Readability rules**

- On pink: white text. Yellow on pink only for the bulb and text 24px and larger (3.2:1).
- On yellow: dark text; logos on yellow are pink.
- On paper: dark text; pink text is fine (4.7:1); yellow is never text on paper.
- Errors are a true red, always with an alert icon and a word: red and pink sit at a similar lightness, so the icon and word tell them apart.
- Every interactive element shows a 2px focus ring.

**Proportions:** product screens are mostly paper (or dark in dark mode), with pink for the main action and brand moments and yellow for the bulb, Bulbs and highlights. Social posts, avatars and packaging are mostly pink.

## 7. Typography

**Alexandria** for Arabic and Latin: one family drawn for both scripts, with every letter Iraqi Arabic and Kurdish use, including gaf, che, pe, zhe and ve (the samples above). Readex Pro, used before, has no gaf, che, pe or zhe, so those letters fell back to another font and looked wrong in words like the brand line. **Geist Mono** for prices, bids, Bulbs amounts and order numbers on screens. The logo's wordmark is Alexandria Bold too.

| Style | Size / line | Weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display XL · Display | 72/76 · 56/62, −2% | 700 |
| Display Arabic | 64 / 88 | 700 |
| H1 · H2 · H3 | 40/48 · 30/38 · 22/30 | 700 · 600 · 600 |
| Body large · Body · Body Arabic | 18/30 · 16/26 · 17/30 | 400 |
| Label · Caption | 14/20 · 12/16 | 600 · 500 |

Never set Arabic below 14px; never letter-space, italicize or fake-bold Arabic; product and model names stay in Latin inside Arabic copy.

## 8. Layout, space and shape

- 4px grid; 1200px max content width; 12 columns on desktop, 4 on phones with 16px gutters.
- RTL is the default: Arabic pages mirror completely; arrows flip; the bulb, numbers, prices, countdowns and media controls do not.
- Corners: 10px controls, 16px cards, 28px hero panels and plan cards, pills for chips and tiles.
- Depth: borders first, a soft shadow for resting cards, a stronger one only for floating layers. No gradients or glows.

## 9. Signature devices

1. **The bulb kicker.** A small yellow bulb (pink on paper) centered above every post headline, slide title and receipt header.
2. **The Highlight.** One or two headline words on a yellow swipe with dark text; on pink grounds, the words simply turn yellow. One per headline.
3. **Bulbs.** Our rewards: 1 Bulb = 1 IQD. The small yellow bulb icon is reserved for Bulbs and nothing else (not condition, not plans). Everyone earns 2%; Plus Glow 3%, Shine 4%, Blaze 5%, on everything except digital codes and Plus fees. Spend them in steps of 250, from 1,000. No tiers, no cash-out, no transfers.
4. **Honest prices.** The price, then, only when it's true, the struck-through "was" price. "Lowest in 30 days" sits under every price on a product page. Student prices and Plus prices are labeled and shown only to those who get them. There is no market or souq price.
5. **Condition labels.** Every copy for sale that isn't new shows one plain outline label: علبة مفتوحة Open box · مجدّد Refurbished · مستعمل Used. New shows no label. No meter, never a bulb; letter grades stay internal; rentals show no condition label.
6. **Plan marks.** The plan's name in pink with its item count: **Plus Glow · 1 item**, **Plus Shine · 2 items**, **Plus Blaze · 4 items**; "Duo" follows when shared ("Plus Shine Duo").
7. **The Pink Band.** One full-width pink band per page with a white headline (one yellow phrase) and a yellow button: the main call to action, also at events and on staff wear.
8. **The Pink Mailer.** Every Plus rental travels in the Pink Mailer (see the Packaging guide).
9. **The Rays.** Short rounded strokes from the bulb's own rays, fanned or ringed, as a pattern on packaging, backdrops and empty states; yellow on pink. One ray pattern per layout.

## 10. Imagery and illustration

- **Catalog products:** cut-out, front-on or three-quarter, centered; on pink for posts and promos, on paper in the product. Soft even light, no props.
- **One-of-a-kind copies** (used items, auctions, collectibles): the seller's own photos of that exact copy, at least 3, in daylight, on a plain ground, showing any wear. Never a stock image in place of the real copy.
- **People:** real Iraqis in real places: Mutanabbi Street, a Karrada café, a campus, a Basra living room. Natural light, candid moments. No stock handshakes.
- **Illustration:** one flat vector style built from the radius scale and the rays: solid shapes in pink, yellow and paper plus skin tones, no gradients or 3D, no clip-art, no mascot. A core set of 30 scenes (exams, travel, study, events, Eid, Ramadan, deadlines, jobs, countries) keeps the Club in one style.
- **Third-party marks and game art** appear only to describe genuine products.

## 12. Product UI and navigation

- **Phone tabs:** Store · Plus · Club · Pass. Hidden during sign-in and checkout.
- **Top bar:** on phones, logo · search · Saved (heart) · bell · cart; on desktop, logo · Store · Plus · Club · search · Saved · bell · cart · Pass. Only the bell (unread) and the cart (items) show counts.
- **Footer:** desktop only, one slim line (For partners · About · Careers · Help · Legal, language and theme). Phones and the apps have none: About & help is the last row of the Pass, and the version and build sit at the bottom of /about.
- **Where things live:** your Pass holds the card, Today and the Library (At home, Queue, History, Saved, Lists, Tickets, Applications, Codes), Family, Bulbs and Settings; /plus holds the Plus shelves, the plans and your plan; /club holds events, opportunities, surveys and Read.
- **Buttons:** the primary action is pink with white text, whatever it is (buy, add to Plus, bid); on pink panels it is yellow with dark text; secondary actions are outlined.
- **States** show in a word with an icon: Price dropped, Back in stock (success) · Out now, Rentable now (pink) · Coming soon, Unavailable (neutral). Auctions show time left in mono; in the last hour they turn to the warning tone with "Ending soon".
- Two themes: Paper (light) and Dark, following the system setting.
- Icons: Lucide, 1.75px stroke, rounded joins, `currentColor`; directional icons flip in RTL. The bulb icon only for Bulbs.
- Motion: things "switch on": a fade with scale 0.96 → 1 in 120–240ms, no bounce; reduced motion means opacity only.
- Every text and control meets WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. Games show their age rating.
