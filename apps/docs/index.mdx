The IDEA Pass is the card of The IDEA in Iraq. Its members are
students, gamers, readers and music lovers who already carry it on their
phones. These docs are for partners who build on it:

## How it fits together

- **The IDEA Partners** (`partners.theideaiq.com`) is where your
  organization registers apps, verifies domains and manages secrets
  (Developers, for owners and admins).
- **The API** (`api.theideaiq.com`) runs the OpenID Connect provider and
  the card endpoints. Its base for sign-in is `https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc`.
- **The website** (`theideaiq.com`) is where people sign in and say yes:
  the consent screen and the live card are on its pages.

Nothing you receive includes orders, payments or anything the person
didn't tick. Every site gets its own id for each person.
