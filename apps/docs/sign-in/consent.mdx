---
title: "Consent"
description: "When people see the consent screen, and how to skip it."
---

The first time someone signs in to your app, The IDEA shows your app's
name, its website and what you ask for. They can untick anything that isn't
required and say yes or no.

After that, The IDEA remembers what they shared with your app:

- **Without `prompt`:** a returning person who already shared everything
  you ask for comes straight back with a code. Ask for something new and
  they see the consent screen again, for the new part.
- **`prompt=consent`** (or `login`): always show the consent screen.
- **`prompt=none`:** never show a page. A person who already shared what
  you ask for comes back with a code; anyone else comes back with
  `error=consent_required`. Use it to sign people in silently, and fall
  back to a normal request on that error.

People see your app in **Pass → Settings → Connected sites** and can
disconnect it there. That ends its refresh tokens and live card frames, and
the next sign-in asks again.
