---
title: "Client authentication"
description: "Your client secret, or signed assertions with your own keys."
---

The token endpoint and the card check take:

- **`client_secret_basic`** (the default): `Authorization: Basic base64(client_id:client_secret)`.
- **`client_secret_post`:** `client_id` and `client_secret` in the body.
- **`private_key_jwt`:** a signed assertion instead of the secret.

Keep the secret on your server. A mobile app calls your own backend, which
calls The IDEA.

## Rotating the secret

In Developers, **Rotate the secret** shows a new one. The previous one keeps
working for 24 hours (or stops at once, if you choose), so you can deploy
the new one first; you can stop it early.

## `private_key_jwt`

Upload your public keys in Developers (a JWK set of up to 5 EC P-256 or
RSA 2048+ keys). Then sign a JWT instead of sending the secret:

```text
client_assertion_type=urn:ietf:params:oauth:client-assertion-type:jwt-bearer
client_assertion=<JWT>
```

The JWT:

- signed with ES256 or RS256;
- `iss` and `sub`: your client id;
- `aud`: the issuer (`https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc`) or the endpoint
  (`…/oidc/token`, `…/oidc/card/check`);
- at most 5 minutes from `iat` to `exp`;
- a fresh `jti` each time (each is accepted once).

The card check takes the same two fields in its JSON body.
