---
title: "Check a Pass card"
description: "Scan the live card's QR code at your door."
---

The live card's QR code reads `IDEA:<member number>:<6 digits>`; the
digits change every 30 seconds. Send what you scanned from your server:

```bash
curl https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc/card/check \
  -u "pass_…:$CLIENT_SECRET" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "code": "IDEA:100007:123456" }'
```

The answer is `{ "valid": false }`, or:

```json
{
  "valid": true,
  "sub": "…",
  "given_name": "Sara",
  "member_number": 100007,
  "student": { "verified": true, "university": "auib", "until": "2027-06-30" },
  "adult": true
}
```

- `sub` is the same id as in your ID tokens, or `null` if the person never
  signed in to your app. Match it to your member.
- `given_name` and `member_number` are printed on the card, so whoever is
  at the door can compare. The yes/no answers are the ones your app is
  registered for.
- Showing the card is the person's consent to the check. Every check is
  listed in their Pass → Settings.
- 30 failed checks in 10 minutes pause your checks for a while (HTTP 429
  with `Retry-After`).
- In testing, only your organization's members' cards pass.

Your client secret stays on your server: a scanning app calls your own
backend, which calls this endpoint. [`private_key_jwt`](/sign-in/client-authentication#private_key_jwt)
works here too.
