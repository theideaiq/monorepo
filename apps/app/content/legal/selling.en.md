This policy explains how selling works on The IDEA: shops selling through The IDEA Store (businesses and individual traders), "Get offers from shops" and auctions for collectibles. It applies alongside our [Terms](/legal/terms). These parts open in stages; each applies as it opens, and until then the page or button for it isn't shown.

## 1. Who sells

Everyone who sells on The IDEA does it as a shop in Partners: a **registered business** (its commercial registration and the owner's ID) or an **individual trader** (the owner's ID only, with a monthly sales limit). A person who wants to sell their own things joins as an individual trader. Buyers pay The IDEA, online with Wayl or in cash on delivery, as for any Store order; The IDEA collects the money as the shop's payment agent, and returns follow [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns).

## 2. Shops: joining

- A shop sells through an organization in Partners (see [Partners](/legal/business)). It applies from Partners → Selling with its business documents, the bank details for its payouts and its security deposit, and starts selling once our staff approve it.
- The people who act for the shop must be allowed to bind it, and keep its details, documents and bank details up to date.
- We may refuse an application, or ask for more information, without having to give a reason.

## 3. Shops: selling in your own name

- A shop sells **in its own name**: it is the seller of each item it lists, responsible for the item, its description, its condition, its warranty and its lawful sale. Its product pages say "Sold by [shop] · delivered and paid through The IDEA".
- The IDEA runs the store, collects payment and arranges delivery, but doesn't buy or own the shop's items.
- Buyers contact The IDEA, not the shop, about their orders; we involve the shop where we need to.

## 4. Shops: listings

- **Accurate descriptions.** Every listing shows what the item is (title, edition, format or platform), its price in IQD, its stock, its condition grade and what comes with it. Copies that aren't new describe any wear or fault and show photos of the actual item where it matters.
- **Condition grades.** Use New, Open box, Refurbished and Used with exactly the meanings in [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns), and check items as described there.
- **Prices** are set by the shop. Each listing shows its price history, so a price can't be raised to make a discount look bigger.
- **Stock** must be real: list only what you have and can send.
- **Age ratings:** show the PEGI rating of every game; PEGI 18 and other mature items are hidden from and not sold to members under 18.
- Items that are not allowed on The IDEA are listed in section 12.

## 5. Shops: orders and shipping

- Shop orders are shipped by **Boxy for The IDEA**: Boxy collects each order from the shop's address, and the buyer tracks it in Pass → Orders as usual (see [Delivery](/legal/delivery)).
- Each branch you ship from is a Boxy pickup location with your pickup contact, set in Partners → Selling → Pickup. The courier calls that contact to collect parcels.
- Pack each order safely and mark it ready for pickup within 2 days in Partners, then print its label. Labels can be printed until the courier collects the parcel. Lines not ready after 2 days go to our team, and may be cancelled and refunded to the buyer.
- If an item turns out to be unavailable, mark it **Out of stock**: the line is cancelled and refunded, and it counts against your shop.
- The label shows the buyer's name, phone and address so the courier can deliver. Use them only to send that parcel: don't contact buyers, keep their details, or sell to them outside The IDEA. Questions go through The IDEA.

## 6. Shops: payment and payouts

- **The IDEA as payment agent.** The IDEA collects buyers' payments on the shop's behalf, through its own arrangements with Wayl and Boxy. A buyer's payment to The IDEA counts as payment to the shop.
- **Weekly payouts.** Each week we pay the shop for the orders whose 14-day return window has closed in that period, by bank transfer from The IDEA to the shop.
- **What is taken off:** our commission, at the rate shown in Partners for each category; refunds and returns on the shop's orders; and any other charge set out in Partners.
- **Bulbs:** when a buyer pays part of a shop's item with Bulbs, the shop still receives the full price; The IDEA credits that part to the shop, set against its commission (see [Rewards](/legal/rewards)).
- **Statements** in Partners show every order, deduction and payout.
- We may hold a payout while an order is disputed or fraud is suspected, and tell the shop why.

## 7. Shops: returns and items not as described

Shops' items follow [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns) in full. When an item is faulty, wrong or not as described, The IDEA refunds the buyer, including delivery, and recovers the amount (and the return delivery) from the shop's security deposit and next payout. Returned items go back to the shop through our courier.

## 8. Shops: security deposit

- A shop pays a security deposit, of the amount shown in Partners, before it starts selling. It covers refunds and other amounts the shop owes The IDEA.
- When part of it is used, the shop tops it up from its next payout or by transfer.
- When a shop stops selling, the deposit is returned once the last return window has closed and nothing is owed.

## 9. Shops: the Pro plan

Selling is free to start; The IDEA takes only its commission. Shops that want more can take the one paid plan, **Pro**. Its price and what it includes are shown in Partners before you subscribe, and set out in [Partners](/legal/business).

## 10. Get offers from shops

Instead of listing an item yourself, you can ask shops on The IDEA for offers. Describe the item, its condition and photos; shops that are interested send offers through The IDEA. You choose one or none. The shop that made the offer you accept is the buyer: The IDEA passes on the offers and messages, but doesn't buy the item, set the price or guarantee the offer. The offer says how the shop checks the item and pays you.

## 11. Auctions for collectibles

Approved shops, and The IDEA for its own copies, can auction collectibles: copies that are signed, limited or rare, products The IDEA lists as collectible, and used copies in the categories open for auctions. New copies at their usual price and rentals aren't auctioned. These rules apply to every auction:

- **The copy and its photos.** Each auction shows the seller's own photos of that exact copy (at least 3) and describes its condition. A seller who marks a copy signed, limited or rare must be right: The IDEA reviews these marks, and a wrong one counts against the shop.
- **Who can bid.** You need to be 18 or over and have a verified phone number. A maximum above 500,000 IQD also needs your identity verified. Sellers, and the people who work for them, can't bid on their own auctions.
- **Proxy bidding.** You enter the most you are willing to pay; The IDEA bids for you, one step at a time, only as far as needed to keep you ahead, up to your maximum. Other bidders don't see your maximum. Bids are shown by number ("Bidder 3"), never by name.
- **Reserve price.** A seller may set a hidden minimum. If bidding doesn't reach it, the item isn't sold. The listing says whether there is a reserve and whether it has been met.
- **No "Buy It Now".** An auction ends only by bidding; an item at a fixed price is a normal listing.
- **Ending.** An auction runs 3, 5, 7 or 10 days. A bid in the **last 2 minutes** adds **2 minutes**, so everyone has time to respond.
- **A winning bid is a promise to buy.** The winner has **48 hours** to pay, online through the cart, where the win is waiting.
- **Strikes.** A win that isn't paid in time is a strike. Two strikes within 12 months pause bidding for 90 days; three end it. The seller may then offer the item once to the next highest bidder at that bidder's last bid; letting that offer pass isn't a strike.
- **Fees.** The seller pays the commission for the item's category on the final price. There are no listing fees.
- **No shill bidding.** Sellers, and anyone acting with them, must not bid on their own items or push up prices. We check bidding patterns, cancel auctions affected by shill bidding and remove those involved.

## 12. Items not allowed

You may not sell, list or auction on The IDEA:

- counterfeit, pirated or copied goods, or copies of games, music, books or films;
- items rented from The IDEA Plus or anyone else, or anything you don't own;
- stolen goods;
- weapons, ammunition and anything made to look like them, drugs, medicines, alcohol and tobacco;
- personal documents, sign-ins to other services, or anyone's personal data;
- recalled or unsafe items, and anything whose sale is unlawful in Iraq.

We may remove any listing that breaks this policy without notice.

## 13. When rules are broken

We may remove listings, cancel orders or auctions, hold or set off payouts, keep the security deposit for amounts owed, and suspend selling or the Pass or organization concerned. Where we can, we tell you why first and give you a chance to respond.
