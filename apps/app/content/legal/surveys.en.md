Where offered, The IDEA Club lets members create surveys and answer other people's. This policy sets the rules for creators and the terms for respondents.

## 1. Who can take part

- Answering and creating surveys needs The IDEA Pass, and you must be 18 or over.
- A survey for people under 18 is allowed only when its creator has ethics approval for younger participants and a guardian consents for each one.
- Student-only surveys need a verified Student badge.

## 2. Two kinds of survey

1. **Free (give to get).** Each survey you answer gives you one response credit for your own survey. Nobody is paid.
2. **Boosted.** The creator pays The IDEA in IQD through an order (no Bulbs, no coupons): the reward plus 10% for each response wanted. The IDEA rewards each counted response with Bulbs: 100 Bulbs a minute of the usual answer time, from 200 to 2,000. Before the survey opens the time is estimated from its questions; after the first 10 counted responses the reward follows their real median time. If it's lower, the difference goes back to the creator when the survey closes; if it's higher, the survey pauses until the creator pays the difference. A Boosted survey opens once it's paid and stays open until it reaches its target or 60 days pass. What it didn't use is refunded to the way the creator paid, from 1,000 IQD.

A survey is one kind or the other, so nobody is rewarded twice. Bulbs are never sold or transferred: creators can't pay respondents with their own Bulbs.

## 3. Rules for creators

- **Say who you are and why.** Every survey shows who runs it and its purpose (for example "Thesis research, AUIB" or "Market research by an organization"), and starts with consent text, in Arabic and English, that names the creator, explains the purpose, says how long it takes, how answers are used and kept, and that taking part is voluntary.
- **Ask only what you need.** Don't ask for names, phone numbers, emails or other contact details. Free-text answers can identify people too: ask for them only when you need them.
- **No sensitive questions** (health, religion or sect, ethnicity, political views, sexual life, criminal records, finances) unless the survey has the approval it needs and explains why they are asked. Such questions are always optional.
- **Academic research** involving people needs the ethics approval your university requires; tell us when you create the survey. Academic surveys on sensitive topics need the ethics reference and are reviewed before they open.
- **Deception studies** (that don't tell respondents everything at first) need the ethics reference and a debrief, which each respondent sees after sending their answers; they are always reviewed.
- **No under-18s** without ethics approval and guardian consent (section 1).
- **No selling or tricks:** surveys must not be advertising, sales, political campaigning, or a way to collect contacts, and must not hide who runs them.
- **Respect anonymity:** if a survey is anonymous, don't try to identify anyone, and don't combine answers with other data to do so.
- **Use answers and exports only** for the purpose the survey states. Keep exported answers (Excel, SPSS or CSV) secure and delete them when you no longer need them.
- **Quotas:** you can cap the share of responses from any one university, major, year or governorate, never by a sensitive attribute.
- **Results summary:** when your survey closes, its respondents see a summary of the choice and scale questions. Only academic surveys that need to can turn it off.

We may review a survey before it goes live, and refuse or remove one that breaks these rules. A creator who breaks them may lose survey access and, for a Boosted survey, the refund of what a survey that broke the rules didn't use. Creators can't reject responses: only the counting rules in section 4 decide. If a creator closes a survey early, everyone who started it in the last 60 minutes is still rewarded in full, at the creator's cost.

## 4. Terms for respondents

- **Targeting:** surveys can be shown to members by university, major, year and governorate. Creators don't see those details about you, only how many matched.
- **Anonymous answers:** when a survey is anonymous, its creator sees your answers without your name or Pass details. When it isn't, the survey says so before you start.
- **What counts.** Before you start, a survey shows its reward, about how long it takes, whether it has check questions, and these rules. A response doesn't count if 2 or more check questions are wrong, or if it's sent in under a third of the usual (median) time (before there is one, under the creator's minimum time). One wrong check question still counts. Rewards never depend on what you answer.
- **Why, and appeals.** If your response doesn't count you see why, and you can appeal once; our team decides. If the appeal is upheld, the response counts and is rewarded.
- **One response per person.** Rewards and credits are given once per survey, when the response counts.
- **Boosted surveys:** answering needs a phone number you verified that no other Pass uses (one Pass per person), and you can answer up to 5 a day. Respondents with 20 counted answers and no failed check question in their last 10 see new Boosted surveys 24 hours early; in that time they can fill at most half of the target, and the early places go round among them.
- **Small groups stay hidden:** no breakdown, filter or summary shows a group of fewer than 5 people, to creators or respondents. Results show who answered next to the fixed note that respondents are The IDEA Pass users who chose to answer, some for a reward, not a random sample of Iraq.
- **Perks:** when a survey closes you see its results summary (unless an academic survey turned it off); counted answers earn a contributor badge on your Pass at 10, 50 and 200; and Club → Yours → Surveys gives a participation certificate (PDF) listing the surveys you answered, their dates and how many, never your answers. A university that gives course credit for answering surveys must offer students another way to earn it. There are no raffles, prize draws or cash.
- **Leaving:** you can stop a survey at any time before sending; unfinished answers aren't counted or rewarded. After sending, you can delete your answers until the survey closes: they stop counting and you keep the reward.

## 5. Data and retention

The IDEA runs the survey tools. Raw answers are deleted 24 months after the survey closes, or later when the survey's ethics approval states a longer period (up to 10 years); the creator is told 30 days before. The counts, "who answered" and the results summary stay. The creator is responsible for how they use the answers they export, for the stated purpose only.
