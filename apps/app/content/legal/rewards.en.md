This policy covers Bulbs, the points of The IDEA Pass, and inviting friends.

## 1. Bulbs

- **Value:** 1 Bulb is worth 1 IQD when you spend it with The IDEA. Bulbs earned before 29 October 2026 were multiplied by 100 when this changed, so they kept their value.
- **No cash value:** Bulbs can't be exchanged for money, sold or moved to another Pass.
- **Where to see them:** Pass → Bulbs shows your balance and history.

## 2. Earning Bulbs

**On purchases**, Bulbs are a flat share of what you pay in money for items, rentals and game night kits (not delivery or deposits), at the store rate or your Plus plan's rate, rounded to whole Bulbs:

| Who | Bulbs back |
| --- | --- |
| Every Pass holder | 2% |
| Plus Glow | 3% |
| Plus Shine | 4% |
| Plus Blaze | 5% |

The rate is the same for every item, shops' items included; product pages show how many Bulbs an item earns. The IDEA Plus payments and survey payments earn no Bulbs. Because at most 20% of an order is given away in discounts and rewards combined, an order with a large discount may earn fewer Bulbs; the checkout shows how many it will earn.

**Bonus Bulbs:**

| For | Bulbs |
| --- | --- |
| Your first order | 2,000 |
| Your birthday (set once in Pass → Settings) | 2,500 |
| A published review with photos of something you bought or rented | 500, once per product |
| Checking in at a Club event | 1,000, once per event |
| Linking Telegram to your Pass | 1,000, the first time |
| A friend you invited, once their first order is delivered | 5,000, up to 20 friends a month |
| Answering a rewarded survey, where offered | 200–1,000, by length, paid by the survey's creator |

Reviews that earned photo Bulbs are labelled as such.

## 3. When Bulbs unlock

Bulbs from an order unlock **14 days after delivery**, once the return window has closed (see [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns)). Until then they show as pending. If the order is cancelled, returned or refunded, the Bulbs it earned are cancelled.

## 4. Spending Bulbs

At checkout you can pay for all or part of an order with unlocked Bulbs, delivery included: at least 1,000 Bulbs, in steps of 250. An order paid in full with Bulbs needs no other payment. If the order is cancelled or refunded, the Bulbs you spent come back. Bulbs can't pay for Plus at the moment.

## 5. Expiry

Bulbs expire after **12 months without activity** on your Pass (earning or spending Bulbs). We remind you after 11 months. Bulbs also end when you delete your Pass.

## 6. Invite friends

- Share your link (theideaiq.com/r/your-code) from Pass → Bulbs. A friend who is new to The IDEA (no paid or confirmed order yet) gets **10% off their first order**, up to 15,000 IQD.
- You earn **5,000 Bulbs** when their first order is delivered; they unlock with the order's return window and are cancelled if it is refunded. Up to 20 friends a month.
- Each person has one inviter, and you can't invite yourself. No reward is paid when the friend's order shares a phone number or address with your own orders.

## 7. Fair use and changes

We may cancel Bulbs or discounts obtained by fraud, by several Passes for one person, or by breaking these rules, and suspend the Pass concerned (see [Terms](/legal/terms)). We may change how Bulbs are earned and the bonuses; we will tell you in advance, and Bulbs you have already earned keep their value.
