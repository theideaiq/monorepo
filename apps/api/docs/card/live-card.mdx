---
title: "The live card on your site"
description: "Frame the member's live Pass card, QR code and all, in your own pages."
---

Members can show their **live** card (the QR code and the 6-digit code
that changes every 30 seconds) inside your pages, without the code ever
reaching your servers. It isn't an OpenID scope and needs no token: ask
for **The live Pass card on your site** in Developers. It works at once in
testing (for your organization's members) and after our review once live.

## Frame it

```html
<iframe
  src="https://theideaiq.com/pass/embed?client_id=pass_…&lang=ar&theme=light"
  title="The IDEA Pass"
  style="width: 100%; max-width: 448px; border: 0"
></iframe>
```

| Parameter | |
| --- | --- |
| `client_id` | Required |
| `lang` | `ar` or `en` (or use `/ar/pass/embed`) |
| `theme` | `light` or `dark`, for the few words around the card (otherwise the browser's) |

The card itself always looks the same; around it the frame is transparent.

## Your page's headers

- **Only your verified domains may frame it** (and their subdomains, over
  https): the frame sends `Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors` built
  from them. Add `frame-src https://theideaiq.com` to your own CSP.
- **Don't send `Cross-Origin-Opener-Policy: same-origin`** on the page with
  the frame: the browser then cuts the sign-in window off from the frame,
  and the card never arrives. Send `same-origin-allow-popups`, or no such
  header, on that page.

## How members connect it

Browsers keep The IDEA's cookie out of other sites' frames, so the frame
shows a **Show my Pass card** button. It opens a small window on
theideaiq.com where the member (signed in, or after signing in) allows
your site; the window hands the frame a token for this browser and closes.
Nothing goes through your page, and no token is ever put in a URL.

The frame keeps that token in its own storage for your site, so the card
shows straight away next time (up to 90 days after it was last shown).
It always shows the card of whoever allowed it in that browser: you can't
choose a card. Members can **Stop** your site in Pass → Settings →
Connected sites, or remove it from the browser in the frame.

## Fitting the height

The frame tells your page its height, and nothing else:

```js
window.addEventListener("message", (event) => {
  if (event.origin === "https://theideaiq.com" && event.data?.type === "idea-pass-card") {
    frame.style.height = `${event.data.height}px`;
  }
});
```

Keep checking scans with [the card check](/card/check), and keep a link to
[theideaiq.com/pass](https://theideaiq.com/pass) as a fallback.
