---
title: "Register an app"
description: "Verify your domain, register, test with your team and go live."
---

Owners and admins register apps in **Partners → Developers**
([partners.theideaiq.com/developers](https://partners.theideaiq.com/developers)).
If your organization doesn't show Developers yet, add it from Home.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Verify a domain">
    Add your domain, then publish the token we show, either:

    - as a DNS TXT record `_theidea-pass.<domain>` with the value
      `theidea-pass-verification=<token>`, or
    - as a file at `https://<domain>/.well-known/theidea-pass-verification.txt`
      holding the token.

    Press **Check now**. We look for the record, then the file (no
    redirects, 5 seconds). theideaiq.com and shared hosting domains can't be
    used.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register the app">
    Its name (shown on the consent screen), website and exact return
    addresses. The website and every https return address must be on a
    verified domain (or under it); `http://localhost:<port>/…` is allowed
    for development. Tick the answers (scopes) it needs.

    You see the **client id** (`pass_…`) and **client secret** once; we keep
    only a hash of the secret.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test with your team">
    A new app is in **testing**: only your organization's members can sign
    in (and only their cards pass card checks), and every answer you
    registered works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go live">
    Everyone can sign in. The basic answers work at once. The sensitive ones
    (`student_email`, `phone`, `birthdate`, `address`, `offline_access` and
    the live card) wait for our review and aren't asked until approved; you
    get a notification either way. Asking for a new sensitive answer later
    goes back to review, while approved ones keep working.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Managing it

- **Secrets:** rotate (the previous secret keeps working 24 hours, or stops
  at once if you choose), stop the previous one early, or upload public keys
  for [`private_key_jwt`](/sign-in/client-authentication#private_key_jwt).
- **People and activity:** the people connected, and the last 30 days'
  sign-ins and card checks.
- **Off switch:** turn the app off at any time; staff can also suspend it.
- **Limits:** up to 10 domains and 10 apps per organization. A domain an app
  still uses can't be removed.
