---
title: "Quickstart"
description: "Sign someone in with The IDEA Pass in five steps."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register your app">
    In [Partners → Developers](https://partners.theideaiq.com/developers),
    verify your domain, then register an app with its name, website and
    return address (for example `https://example.iq/auth/callback`). Copy the
    **client id** (`pass_…`) and **client secret**: the secret is shown once.
    See [Register an app](/apps/register).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the person to The IDEA">
    Make a PKCE verifier and its S256 challenge, keep the verifier with a
    random `state` and `nonce`, then redirect:

    ```text
    https://theideaiq.com/oauth/authorize
      ?response_type=code
      &client_id=pass_…
      &redirect_uri=https://example.iq/auth/callback
      &scope=openid profile email
      &state=…&nonce=…
      &code_challenge=…&code_challenge_method=S256
    ```

    The person signs in to The IDEA Pass (or already is), sees your app's
    name and what you ask for, and says yes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive the code">
    We send them back to your return address with `code`, `state` and `iss`
    (`https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc`). Check that `state` is yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Exchange it for tokens">
    From your server:

    ```bash
    curl https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc/token \
      -u "pass_…:$CLIENT_SECRET" \
      -d grant_type=authorization_code \
      -d code="$CODE" \
      -d redirect_uri=https://example.iq/auth/callback \
      -d code_verifier="$VERIFIER"
    ```

    The answer has an `id_token`, an `access_token` and the `scope` the
    person actually shared.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify the ID token">
    Check its signature with the keys at
    `https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc/jwks` (ES256), that `iss` is
    `https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc`, `aud` is your client id and `nonce`
    is yours. `sub` is the person's id **at your site**: store it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Any OpenID Connect library works: point it at the discovery document,
`https://api.theideaiq.com/oidc/.well-known/openid-configuration`.

<Tip>
  A new app is in **testing**: only members of your own organization can
  sign in. Test with them, then go live from Developers.
</Tip>
