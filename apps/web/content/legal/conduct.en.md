## 1. Our pledge

At The IDEA we believe in inclusivity, kindness and a positive community for everyone. We pledge to make taking part in our programs, events and spaces a harassment-free experience for all, regardless of age, appearance, disability, ethnicity, gender, level of experience, language, nationality, religion or social background.

## 2. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- all online spaces run by The IDEA: our website and apps, The Club's rooms on Telegram, our social media and Droid;
- all in-person events, workshops, Academy programs and exchanges;
- everyone taking part: members, customers, staff, volunteers, partners, sponsors and guest speakers.

In The Club's rooms, our [Community guidelines](/legal/community) also apply.

## 3. Expected behaviour

- **Be inclusive:** use welcoming language and make room for others.
- **Be respectful:** respect different viewpoints and experiences, and accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- **Be considerate:** show empathy towards other members of the community.
- **Respect boundaries:** respect personal space, and stop when someone asks you to. Consent can be withdrawn at any time; once it is, the conversation or interaction must end.

## 4. Unacceptable behaviour

- **Harassment:** public or private harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted following.
- **Sexual misconduct:** unwelcome sexual attention, language or imagery.
- **Violence and threats:** threats of violence, incitement or inflammatory rhetoric.
- **Doxing:** publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- **Discrimination:** insulting or excluding people for who they are.

## 5. Reporting

If you are being harassed, see someone else being harassed or have any other concern, contact The IDEA's team straight away:

- by email: [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com);
- at an event: speak to any member of our staff or the organizers;
- on Telegram: send a message to our team through Droid (@TheIdeaDroidBot).

All reports are taken seriously, investigated and handled in confidence.

## 6. Enforcement

The IDEA may take any action it considers appropriate to stop unacceptable behaviour, including:

- a verbal or written warning;
- removal from an event or program, without refund;
- suspension or permanent removal from our online communities or the Service;
- reporting the incident to the authorities.

## 7. Accessibility at events

We want our venues to be welcoming to everyone.

- **Mobility:** if you need step-free access, tell the organizers in advance so we can plan for it.
- **Language:** our events are usually held in Arabic, English or both; ask the organizers if you need help following.
- **Photography:** we may take photos at events. Let the organizers know if you don't want to be photographed.

## 8. Alcohol and drugs

Alcohol is not served at The IDEA's programs and events. Using or promoting illegal drugs is strictly forbidden and leads to immediate removal.

## 9. Academic integrity (the Academy)

We value honesty above all. Plagiarism, cheating in assessments or presenting someone else's work as your own leads to removal from the program without refund. Certificates are earned, not bought. The same applies to the Journal: only submit work that is your own.

## 10. Respect for our staff and delivery team

Our delivery team and support staff work hard to serve you. Aggression, threats or harassment directed at them, in chat, by phone or at your door, lead to an immediate, permanent ban and may lead to legal action.
