The IDEA Partners serves companies, universities and other organisations: buying, The IDEA Plus for their teams and partner perks for The IDEA Pass holders. It runs at partners.theideaiq.com and is described on [/partners](/partners). These terms apply alongside our [Terms](/legal/terms). A signed agreement between your organisation and The IDEA takes precedence over them where the two differ.

## 1. Your organisation

- A person creates the organisation with their own The IDEA Pass and confirms that they may act for it. The organisation is responsible for its members' activity in The IDEA Partners and for paying its quotes, orders, invoices and seats.
- **Roles:** owners and admins manage the organisation, its members and requests; members see its requests, quotes, orders and invoices. An organisation must always have an owner.
- Keep your company details and billing contacts up to date.

## 2. Quotes, purchase orders and invoices

1. **Request:** owners and admins send a request for what they need, with an optional budget and date.
2. **Quote:** we answer with one or more quotes of priced lines. Prices are in IQD unless the quote says otherwise, and a quote is valid until the date it shows.
3. **Purchase order:** accepting a quote (with your own PO reference if you use one) makes it a purchase order; the other quotes on the request are withdrawn.
4. **Delivery:** we deliver as the quote states, and mark the order delivered.
5. **Invoice:** we issue an invoice, due within 30 days unless the quote or your agreement says otherwise. Quotes, orders and invoices can be printed or saved as PDF.

Goods are covered by [Condition and warranty](/legal/condition) and, unless your agreement says otherwise, [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns). If an invoice is overdue, we may hold new orders and seats until it is paid.

## 3. The IDEA Plus for your team

- Owners and admins ask for a monthly Plus plan (a seat) for a member. Once we approve it, the member gets The IDEA Plus and your organisation is invoiced for the first month.
- Seats renew monthly and are invoiced to your organisation. Ending a seat, or the member leaving the organisation, lets the paid month run out.
- The member's rentals follow [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus); your organisation pays for damage or loss only if your agreement says so.

## 4. Partner perks

Partners can offer a perk (a discount or a free extra) to The IDEA Pass holders: everyone, verified Students or Plus members.

- You set the perk and its conditions with us; we list it on The IDEA Pass and in the Club feed.
- Honour the perk for any valid card, as listed. Check cards by scanning them in your dashboard on The IDEA Partners; the scan shows only whether the card is valid and the perk applies, not the member's personal details.
- You see a monthly count of redemptions. Don't record, copy or use members' details from a card for anything else.
- Either side can end a perk with notice; we remove it from the listing.

## 5. Events, kits and surveys

Organisations can book game night kits for events and run rewarded surveys. Kits follow [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus), and surveys follow [Surveys](/legal/surveys).

## 6. Confidentiality and data

Each side keeps the other's non-public information (prices in quotes and documents) confidential. We process members' data as described in [Privacy](/legal/privacy).

## 7. Ending

Your organisation can stop using The IDEA Partners at any time; open purchase orders, invoices and seats must still be settled. An owner can delete the organisation from its settings once nothing is left open. We may suspend an organisation that breaks these terms or doesn't pay.
