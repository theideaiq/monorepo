The IDEA Partners is where companies, universities, shops and other organizations work with The IDEA: partner perks, bulk buying, The IDEA Plus for their teams, apps that use Sign in with The IDEA Pass, selling on The IDEA Store, lending copies to The IDEA Plus and posting opportunities in The IDEA Club. It runs at partners.theideaiq.com and is described on [/partners](/partners). It is **free for organizations**; the only paid plan is Pro, for shops (section 7), and featuring an opportunity is a paid extra (section 9). These terms apply alongside our [Terms](/legal/terms). A signed agreement between your organization and The IDEA takes precedence over them where the two differ.

## 1. Your organization

- A person creates the organization with their own The IDEA Pass and confirms that they may act for it. The organization is responsible for its members' activity in The IDEA Partners and for paying its quotes, orders, invoices, seats and any paid plan or extra.
- **Roles:** owners and admins manage the organization, its members, its sections and requests; members see its requests, quotes, orders and invoices. An organization must always have an owner.
- **Sections:** an organization adds what it needs from Home (Perks, Bulk buying, Developers, Selling, Supply, Opportunities). Some need our approval before they open, as explained below. These parts open in stages; each applies as it opens.
- Keep your organization's details and billing contacts up to date.

## 2. Partner perks

Partners can offer a perk (a discount or a free extra) to The IDEA Pass holders: everyone, verified Students or Plus members.

- You set the perk and its conditions with us; we list it on The IDEA Pass and in The IDEA Club.
- Honour the perk for any valid card, as listed. Check cards by scanning them in Partners → Perks; the scan shows only whether the card is valid and the perk applies, not the member's personal details.
- You see a monthly count of redemptions. Don't record, copy or use members' details from a card for anything else.
- Either side can end a perk with notice; we remove it from the listing.

## 3. Bulk buying

1. **Request:** owners and admins send a request for what they need, with an optional budget and date.
2. **Quote:** we answer with one or more quotes of priced lines. Prices are in IQD unless the quote says otherwise, and a quote is valid until the date it shows.
3. **Purchase order:** accepting a quote (with your own PO reference if you use one) makes it a purchase order; the other quotes on the request are withdrawn.
4. **Delivery:** we deliver as the quote states, and mark the order delivered.
5. **Invoice:** we issue an invoice, due within 30 days unless the quote or your agreement says otherwise. Quotes, orders and invoices can be printed or saved as PDF.

Goods are covered by [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns), including its condition grades and warranty, unless your agreement says otherwise. If an invoice is overdue, we may hold new orders and seats until it is paid.

## 4. The IDEA Plus for your team

- Owners and admins ask for a monthly Plus plan (a seat) for a member. Once we approve it, the member gets The IDEA Plus and your organization is invoiced for the first month.
- Seats renew monthly and are invoiced to your organization. Ending a seat, or the member leaving the organization, lets the paid month run out.
- The member's rentals follow [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus); your organization pays for damage or loss only if your agreement says so.
- Organizations can also book game night kits for their events, under [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus).

## 5. Developers: Sign in with The IDEA Pass

Organizations can let people sign in to their own websites and apps with The IDEA Pass, and check Pass cards from them. The developer docs are at api.theideaiq.com.

- **Registering an app:** in Partners → Developers, verify your domain, register the app, test it with your own members and then go live. Our staff review apps that ask for sensitive details before they can go live, and may suspend any app.
- **Consent:** your app receives only the details each person agrees to share, item by item, on our consent screen. People can disconnect your app at any time in Pass → Settings.
- **Using the details:** use them only for the purpose you registered, keep them secure, keep your app's secrets private, and delete a person's details when they disconnect or you no longer need them.
- **Partner bots:** where offered, organizations can run their own Telegram bot through The IDEA Droid, managed from Partners → Developers. Each bot's data is kept separate to its organization.

## 6. Shops: selling on The IDEA

Shops sell on The IDEA Store in their own name, with The IDEA collecting payment as their payment agent and paying them weekly after the 14-day return window, minus commission. Joining, listings, shipping, payouts, the security deposit, returns and the items that may not be sold are set out in [Selling](/legal/selling).

- **Commission** is the rate shown in Partners → Selling for each category, at the time of the order.
- Where offered, shops can also offer **single-item rentals** of their own items, with their own deposit and ID check, shown on the listing.

## 7. Shops: the Pro plan

Pro is the one paid plan in The IDEA Partners, for shops.

- Its price, billing period and what it includes are shown in Partners → Selling before you subscribe.
- It renews until you cancel; cancelling lets the paid period run out. We don't refund part of a period.
- If we change what Pro includes or costs, we tell you before your next renewal.

## 8. Suppliers: lending copies to The IDEA Plus

Supplier shops can lend copies to the pool The IDEA Plus rents from.

- You choose which copies to lend in Partners → Supply; we check each one when it comes in, as for our own stock (see [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns)).
- Lent copies stay yours. The IDEA rents them to Plus members under [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus) and looks after them while they are in the pool.
- **Fees:** for each copy we owe you either a fee per rental or a monthly fee, as agreed for it in Partners → Supply, where you can follow what is owed and paid.
- What happens if a lent copy is damaged or lost, and how to take copies back, is set out in Partners → Supply before you lend.

## 9. Posting opportunities

Organizations can post jobs, internships, scholarships, competitions and programs from Partners → Opportunities. They appear in The IDEA Club, with your organization named as the one running them.

- **Review:** our staff review each opportunity before it appears, and may ask for changes, refuse it or remove it later.
- **Accurate and fair:** describe the opportunity truthfully, with its real requirements, deadline and any cost to applicants. Don't ask applicants to pay to apply unless the listing says so clearly and we agreed it.
- **Applicants' details** you receive are for that opportunity only; handle them as described in [Privacy](/legal/privacy).
- **Featuring** an opportunity, so it shows more prominently, is a paid extra. Its price is shown before you choose it, and featured opportunities are labelled.

The IDEA's own programs follow [Programs](/legal/programs).

## 10. Surveys

Organizations can run rewarded surveys, following [Surveys](/legal/surveys).

## 11. Confidentiality and data

Each side keeps the other's non-public information (prices in quotes, commission and fees, and documents) confidential. We process members' data as described in [Privacy](/legal/privacy).

## 12. Ending

Your organization can stop using The IDEA Partners at any time; open purchase orders, invoices, seats, payouts, deposits and lent copies must still be settled. An owner can delete the organization from its settings once nothing is left open. We may suspend an organization that breaks these terms or doesn't pay.
