This policy explains the condition grades on The IDEA Store, the warranty each comes with, and how to cancel an order, return an item and get a refund, whether the item is sold by The IDEA or by a shop on The IDEA. It also covers refunds for The IDEA Plus, single-item rentals, game night kits, events and programs. It does not limit the rights you have under Iraq's Consumer Protection Law (Law No. 1 of 2010), including returning goods when you were not given full and correct information about them.

## 1. Who sells the item

Every product page names its seller. Items sold by The IDEA say so; items sold by a shop say "Sold by [shop] · delivered and paid through The IDEA". This policy applies in the same way to both: you pay The IDEA, you contact The IDEA, and The IDEA handles your return and refund. Items people sell to each other work differently (see section 12).

## 2. Condition grades

Every copy has one of four condition grades, shown on the product page before you buy.

| Condition | What it means | Typical price on The IDEA's own listings |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Unused and in its original packaging, sealed where the maker seals it. | The full price |
| **Open box** | The packaging has been opened (for example a display or returned item), but the item is unused or as good as unused and complete. | At most 75% of new |
| **Refurbished** | Used before, then checked, cleaned and repaired where needed by the seller or the maker, and working fully. | About 80–85% of new |
| **Used** | Owned before, with the signs of use described on the product page. Works as it should. | About 50–65% of new, by wear |

When a copy isn't new, its product page describes anything you should know, such as wear on a sleeve, a missing manual or a mark on a case. Photos of collector pieces show the actual item where we can. Shops set their own prices, but must use these grades with the same meaning.

## 3. How items are checked

- **Every copy The IDEA sells** is checked when it comes in: that it is genuine, complete and matches its listing.
- **Open box, Refurbished and Used** items sold by The IDEA are also tested: discs and records for playback and scratches, devices for power, controls and connections, books for missing or damaged pages.
- **Shops** must check their items the same way and describe them accurately (see [Selling](/legal/selling)). We check shops' listings and act on every report.
- Counterfeit or pirated games, records, films or electronics are not sold on The IDEA, by us or by shops.
- **Vintage hi-fi and cameras** sold by The IDEA are one-off pieces; you can view them by appointment before buying.

## 4. Cancelling an order

- **Before payment:** cancel any order awaiting payment in Pass → Orders.
- **Paid, or cash, and not yet sent:** contact us with your order number. If it hasn't been handed to the courier, we cancel it and refund you in full.
- **Already sent:** refuse the parcel (see [Delivery](/legal/delivery)) or return it as below.

## 5. The 14-day window

You can return an item within **14 days of delivery** (or of pickup, where offered). The item must come back with its accessories, packaging and anything that came with it, in the condition you received it.

| Item | Can it be returned within 14 days? |
| --- | --- |
| New, unopened items | Yes, for any reason |
| Opened games, music (records, CDs, cassettes) and films | Only if faulty or not as described |
| Opened earphones and in-ear headphones | Only if faulty or not as described |
| Used, Open box and Refurbished items | Yes, if they are not as described, as well as when faulty |
| Any item that is faulty, wrong or damaged on arrival | Yes |

Games, music and films can't be returned once opened unless they are faulty or not as described, because they can be copied.

## 6. Faulty, wrong or damaged items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged or isn't what you ordered, tell us as soon as you can, within 14 days of delivery, with photos if possible. We will replace it, repair it or refund it in full, including the delivery fee, and collect it at no cost to you. After the 14 days, a faulty item is covered by its warranty, if it has one (section 8).

## 7. Items not as described

"Not as described" means the item differs from what its product page said about it, including its condition grade, its edition or format, what is included, or the wear and faults the page described.

- **Tell us within 14 days of delivery**, with photos or a video showing the difference.
- **You get a full refund, including the delivery fee,** and we collect the item at no cost to you. If you prefer and a matching copy is available, we can replace it instead.
- **On items sold by shops,** the refund comes from The IDEA, to the way you paid, on the same timing as any other refund. We then recover it from the shop's security deposit and next payout; you don't need to deal with the shop.
- This refund is part of every order paid through The IDEA. It isn't a separate guarantee with its own name or price, and there is nothing extra to buy.

## 8. Warranty

| Condition | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| New | The maker's warranty, where one exists, as shown on the product page. |
| Open box | The maker's warranty where it still applies; otherwise the warranty shown on the product page. |
| Refurbished | The seller's own warranty, for the period shown on the product page. |
| Used | No warranty beyond the 14-day return window, unless the product page says otherwise. |

Iraq's Consumer Protection Law requires a warranty for goods that need one; where it does, the product page states it. Every order comes with a receipt showing the seller, price, date and specifications of what you bought; keep it for warranty claims.

**To make a warranty claim,** contact us with your order number and a description or video of the fault. We will repair, replace or refund the item, or help you claim from the maker. On a shop's item, the shop gives the warranty shown on its listing; we pass your claim to the shop and follow it up until it is settled. A warranty doesn't cover damage from accidents, misuse, liquid or unauthorised repairs.

## 9. How to return an item

1. Contact us from [Help](/help#contact), by email or through The IDEA Droid, with your order number, the item and the reason.
2. We tell you how to send it back: collected by our courier, or brought to The IDEA Space where that is offered. Please don't send items back without contacting us first, and don't send a shop's item to the shop directly.
3. We check the item when it arrives and tell you the result.

**Who pays the return delivery:** you do, unless the item is faulty, wrong or not as described; then we do.

## 10. Refunds

Once a return is approved, we refund the price of the item, and the delivery fee when the item was faulty, wrong or not as described.

- **Paid online with Wayl:** the refund goes back to the card or wallet you paid with. Your bank or wallet may take a few days more to show it.
- **Paid in cash:** we refund you in cash or by transfer, as agreed with you.
- **As Bulbs, if you choose:** instead of money, you can take the refund as Bulbs (1 Bulb = 100 IQD), usable right away.
- **Bulbs you spent** on the order come back as Bulbs.
- **Bulbs the order earned** are cancelled, and so are any invite rewards on it (see [Rewards](/legal/rewards)).

We aim to send refunds within 10 business days of approving them.

## 11. Items from shops

Items sold by shops on The IDEA follow this whole policy: the same 14-day window, the same condition grades and the same refunds. The IDEA collects your payment on the shop's behalf and pays the shop only after the return window has closed, so a refund never depends on the shop paying you back.

## 12. Items bought from people

Items that people sell to each other on The IDEA are paid to the seller in person at The IDEA Space or in cash on delivery; the money never passes through The IDEA. This policy's returns and refunds therefore don't apply to them: check the item before you pay, or book a "Checked at the Space" inspection where offered. If a person's item was misdescribed, report it to us; we will help you and the seller resolve it and may remove the seller. See [Selling](/legal/selling).

## 13. The IDEA Plus

- You can cancel any time; the plan stays active until the end of the month (or year) you paid for, once your items are back. We don't refund part of a month.
- If you were charged by mistake or twice, contact us and we will refund the extra charge.
- If a rented item is faulty when it reaches you, tell us within 3 days and we will replace it at no cost.

Details are in [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus).

## 14. Single-item rentals, game night kits and deposits

- A single-item rental or a game night kit can be cancelled for a full refund until it is handed to you or to the rider.
- A refundable deposit is returned in full when the item or kit comes back complete and undamaged, less any charges for late return, damage or loss explained to you (see [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus)).

## 15. Events and programs

The refund terms of a paid event are shown when you book. If we cancel an event, you get a full refund. The IDEA's programs (exchanges, trips and courses) have their own refund schedule, including refunds when a visa is refused (see [Programs](/legal/programs)).

## 16. Organizations

Orders by organizations through The IDEA Partners follow this policy, unless your agreement with us says otherwise (see [The IDEA Partners](/legal/business)).

## 17. Market prices and age ratings

- **Market ("souq") prices.** Some product pages compare a price with the price in Iraq's market (the souq). We show a market price only when we have checked it in the last 7 days and saved the evidence (where and when it was seen). If we haven't, we don't show one.
- **Age ratings.** Games show their PEGI age rating (3, 7, 12, 16 or 18). Games rated PEGI 18 and other mature items are hidden from and not sold to members under 18, by The IDEA or by shops.
