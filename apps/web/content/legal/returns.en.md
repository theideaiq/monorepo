This policy explains how to cancel an order, return an item and get a refund from The IDEA Store, and how refunds work for The IDEA Plus, single-item rentals, game night kits and events. It does not limit the rights you have under Iraq's Consumer Protection Law (Law No. 1 of 2010), including returning goods when you were not given full and correct information about them.

## 1. Cancelling an order

- **Before payment:** cancel any order awaiting payment in Pass → Orders.
- **Paid, or cash, and not yet sent:** contact us with your order number. If it hasn't been handed to the courier, we cancel it and refund you in full.
- **Already sent:** refuse the parcel (see [Delivery](/legal/delivery)) or return it as below.

## 2. The 14-day window

You can return an item within **14 days of delivery** (or of pickup from our store). The item must come back with its accessories, packaging and anything that came with it, in the condition you received it.

| Item | Can it be returned within 14 days? |
| --- | --- |
| New, unopened items | Yes, for any reason |
| Opened games, music (records, CDs, cassettes) and films | Only if faulty |
| Opened earphones and in-ear headphones | Only if faulty |
| Used, Open box and Refurbished items | Yes, if they are not as described, as well as when faulty |
| Any item that is faulty, wrong or damaged on arrival | Yes |

Games, music and films can't be returned once opened unless they are faulty, because they can be copied. "Not as described" means the item differs from what its product page said about it, including its condition (see [Condition and warranty](/legal/condition)).

## 3. Faulty, wrong or damaged items

If an item arrives faulty, damaged or isn't what you ordered, tell us as soon as you can, within 14 days of delivery, with photos if possible. We will replace it, repair it or refund it in full, including the delivery fee, and collect it at no cost to you. After the 14 days, a faulty item is covered by its warranty, if it has one (see [Condition and warranty](/legal/condition)).

## 4. How to return an item

1. Contact us from [Help](/help#contact), by email or through The IDEA Droid, with your order number, the item and the reason.
2. We tell you how to send it back: by courier, or by bringing it to our store where pickup is offered. Please don't send items back without contacting us first.
3. We check the item when it arrives and tell you the result.

**Who pays the return delivery:** you do, unless the item is faulty, wrong or not as described; then we do.

## 5. Refunds

Once a return is approved, we refund the price of the item, and the delivery fee when the return is our fault.

- **Paid online with Wayl:** the refund goes back to the card or wallet you paid with. Your bank or wallet may take a few days more to show it.
- **Paid in cash:** we refund you in cash or by transfer, as agreed with you.
- **As Bulbs, if you choose:** instead of money, you can take the refund as Bulbs (1 Bulb = 100 IQD), usable right away.
- **Bulbs you spent** on the order come back as Bulbs.
- **Bulbs the order earned** are cancelled, and so are any invite or affiliate rewards on it (see [Rewards](/legal/rewards)).

We aim to send refunds within 10 business days of approving them.

## 6. The IDEA Plus

- You can cancel any time; the plan stays active until the end of the month (or year) you paid for, once your items are back. We don't refund part of a month.
- If you were charged by mistake or twice, contact us and we will refund the extra charge.
- If a rented item is faulty when it reaches you, tell us within 3 days and we will replace it at no cost.

Details are in [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus).

## 7. Single-item rentals, game night kits and deposits

- A single-item rental or a game night kit can be cancelled for a full refund until it is handed to you or to the rider.
- A refundable deposit is returned in full when the item or kit comes back complete and undamaged, less any charges for late return, damage or loss explained to you (see [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus)).

## 8. Events

The refund terms of a paid event are shown when you book. If we cancel an event, you get a full refund.

## 9. The IDEA Business

Orders through The IDEA Business follow this policy, unless your agreement with us says otherwise (see [The IDEA Business](/legal/business)).
