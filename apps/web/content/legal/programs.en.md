This policy covers The IDEA's own programs (exchanges, trips and courses) that you book through The IDEA Club, and how other organizations' programs are listed there. It applies alongside our [Terms](/legal/terms). Each program's page gives its own dates, price, ages and details; where a program page and this policy differ, the program page applies to that program.

## 1. Who runs a program

Opportunities in Club → Join each name who runs them.

- **The IDEA's programs** are run and sold by The IDEA, and this whole policy applies to them.
- **Partners' programs, jobs, internships, scholarships and competitions** are run by the partner that posted them, under its own terms (section 11).
- **Free programs run by others,** such as IYLEP and UWC, are listed for information only (section 12).

## 2. What a package includes

The IDEA's programs are sold as whole packages for one price. The program page lists exactly what is included, such as:

- travel and transfers, accommodation and the meals stated;
- the program's classes, visits and activities;
- **travel insurance** for the length of the program;
- an adult **chaperone** travelling with every group of participants under 18;
- help with visa applications, where the program needs one.

Anything not listed (for example a passport, personal spending money, or visa fees where the page says so) isn't included.

## 3. Who can apply

- You need The IDEA Pass, and must fit the ages and any other requirements on the program page.
- **Under 18s** need their guardian's approval from the guardian's own Pass (see [Terms](/legal/terms)). The guardian approves the application and each payment, and signs the consent forms the program needs, such as for travel and medical care.
- Places are limited. Sending an application doesn't guarantee a place; you have a place once we accept you and the first payment is made.

## 4. Applying

- **Details from your Pass.** Your application is filled in with details you have already given The IDEA Pass (such as your name, date of birth, contact details and student badge). You check and correct them before sending; nothing is sent without you.
- The program may ask for more, such as a statement, a reference, school results or medical needs. Give true and complete information: false information can cost you your place.

## 5. Passports and documents

- Programs that travel ask for a **scan of your passport** (and your guardian's ID for under 18s).
- Each scan is **kept with that application only**, and used only for that program: visas, tickets, accommodation and insurance. We share it only with those who need it for those purposes, such as an embassy, airline, host or insurer.
- Scans are **deleted after the program ends**. If you don't take part, they are deleted once your application is closed. See [Privacy](/legal/privacy).

## 6. Price and installments

- The price is in IQD, for the whole package, and shown before you apply.
- You can pay in full, or in **installments** where the program offers them. The installment schedule (how many, how much and the due dates) is shown before you accept a place and is confirmed in Pass → Orders. Every installment is due before the program starts.
- We remind you before each due date.
- **If an installment is missed,** we tell you and give you time to pay, as shown in the schedule. If it is still unpaid after that, we may cancel your place; we then refund what you paid according to the refund schedule (section 7), as if you had cancelled on the date the installment was due.

## 7. If you cancel

Each program page shows a **refund schedule by notice period**: the earlier before the start date you cancel, the more you get back. The schedule is fixed when you accept your place and is shown in Pass → Orders. To cancel, write to us; your notice counts from the day we receive it.

## 8. If your visa is refused

If your visa is refused, send us the proof of refusal. We refund what you paid, **minus the costs that we have actually paid and can't recover** (such as the visa fee or a non-refundable ticket). We show you what we took off and why, with receipts.

## 9. If The IDEA cancels or changes a program

- We may cancel a program, for example if too few people join, a partner or host withdraws, or it is no longer safe or possible to travel. If we cancel, you get a **full refund** of everything you paid us.
- If we must make a significant change (such as the dates or the destination), we tell you at once, and you can accept it or cancel for a full refund.

## 10. During the program

Participants follow the program's rules, the chaperone's and organisers' instructions, the law of the country they are in, and our [Club rules](/legal/club). A participant who seriously breaks them may be asked to leave the program, as explained in its rules.

## 11. Partners' opportunities

Partners post jobs, internships, scholarships, competitions and programs from The IDEA Partners; our staff review each one before it appears. The partner runs it and decides who takes part, and its own terms apply. Featured opportunities are labelled. If a partner's opportunity doesn't match its listing, tell us (see [The IDEA Partners](/legal/business)).

## 12. Free programs run by others

Some free programs, such as IYLEP and UWC, are listed so you can find them. For these:

- **The IDEA charges nothing** and isn't their organiser; we don't select participants or decide applications.
- **Notify me** sends you a reminder from The IDEA when applications open or a deadline is near. You apply on the organiser's own website, under its rules.
