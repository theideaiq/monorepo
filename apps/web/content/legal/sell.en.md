Sell to us lets you sell or trade in records, CDs, games, books, films, consoles and other items to The IDEA. This policy explains who can sell, how offers work, how you are paid and what happens to items we don't buy.

## 1. Who can sell

Sell to us is for The IDEA Pass holders aged 18 and over.

## 2. What we accept

We accept items like the ones we sell: music, games, books, films, audio and tech in working order. We don't accept counterfeit or pirated items, copies made at home, items with someone else's personal data still on them, or anything that is illegal to sell. We may decline any item without giving a reason.

## 3. Proof of ownership

You must own the item and have the right to sell it. By sending a request you confirm this. We may ask for a receipt, the original box or your ID, especially for consoles, phones and other devices. We refuse items we believe are stolen, and may report them to the authorities.

## 4. How it works

1. **Send a request** from a product page or Pass → Orders (Sell to us), with photos and a description of the item and its condition.
2. **We review it** and make an offer, or decline. You get the offer in Pass → Orders, by email and, if you turned them on, on WhatsApp or Telegram.
3. **You accept or decline** the offer. An offer is valid for the time shown with it.
4. **Hand over the item** as we arrange with you: at our store or collected by us.
5. **We check the item.** If it matches your request, the sale is completed. If it doesn't (for example a scratched disc or missing parts), we make a new offer, which you can accept or decline.

## 5. Payment

Once the sale is completed, choose how to be paid:

- **Money,** in cash at handover or by transfer, as agreed with you; or
- **Store credit as Bulbs** worth **120%** of the offer, credited to your Pass at once (1 Bulb = 100 IQD).

Every completed request also earns **10 bonus Bulbs** (see [Rewards](/legal/rewards)). Once you are paid, the item belongs to The IDEA, and a completed sale can't be undone.

## 6. Your data on devices

Before handing over a console, phone, laptop or other device, sign out of your own services, remove your memory cards and reset it to factory settings. We reset every device we buy as well, but we aren't responsible for data you leave on it.

## 7. Declined items

If you decline our offer, or we decline the item after checking it, we return it to you the way it came: you collect it from our store, or we send it back. Please collect it within 30 days of our message. If we can't return it because we can't reach you, we keep it safely for a reasonable time and contact you again before doing anything else with it, as the law allows.

## 8. What we do with items we buy

We may resell them as Used, Refurbished or Open box items (see [Condition and warranty](/legal/condition)), rent them through The IDEA Plus or keep them. Photos you sent are kept with the request for a short time and are not used to sell the item.
