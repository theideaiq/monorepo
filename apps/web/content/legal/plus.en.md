The IDEA Plus is our rental plan: keep games, books and films as long as you like, swap them for the next one, with no due dates and no late fees. This policy also covers renting a single item from The IDEA Store without a Plus plan, and game night kits.

## 1. Where Plus is available

Plus delivers in Baghdad. Elsewhere in Iraq, and when all places are taken, you can join the waitlist on [/plus](/plus) and we will tell you when a place opens.

## 3. Joining and paying

- Join from [/plus](/plus). The plan is billed in advance each month (or year) through **Wayl**: a few days before each renewal we send the payment link, until you cancel. Paying in cash is possible only with a verified Pass.
- **Bulbs:** in Your Plus, choose "Use Bulbs on my next renewal" to pay up to half of a month with Bulbs, in steps of 250. Plus fees don't earn Bulbs; your plan's rate applies to everything else you buy and rent (see [Rewards](/legal/rewards)).
- **Plus as a gift:** prepaid months of a plan can be sent with Send as a gift. They start Plus for the person (when a place is free) or pay their next renewals.
- If a renewal payment fails, we try again and remind you. Until it is paid we may hold new rentals and swaps.
- Members aged 13 to 17 need a guardian to approve Plus from the guardian's own Pass.
- For rentals we may ask for a verified Pass (identity checked with a national ID card or passport).

- **Plus Duo:** Glow Duo 20,750, Shine Duo 31,750 and Blaze Duo 55,750 IQD a month (yearly: pay 10 months, get 12; 20% off when the plan holder is a verified student). The plan holder pays and invites one more Pass from Your Plus with a link that works for 7 days. The second person must be 13 or over and have no Plus of their own; at 13 to 17 their guardian approves it from their own Pass. They get the plan's Bulbs rate, survey credits, early access, Plus prices, partner perks and the Plus badge on their own Pass, and keep their own queue. The plan's items at a time and home swaps are shared, and home swaps go to the plan holder's Plus address; swaps take turns between the two queues unless the plan holder chooses otherwise. The second person can leave at any time; the plan holder can remove them from the next renewal once their rentals are returned. Items the second person still has at the shared address stay with the plan holder. A Duo can be sent with Send as a gift.

## 4. How renting works

- Add what you want to your **queue** (Saved → Queue), in order. When you book a home swap, the next items in it are picked for you; change them if you like.
- **Keep items as long as you like.** There are no due dates and no late fees while your plan is active.
- **Swaps:** our rider brings your next item and takes the last one back in one visit, on your area's delivery day.
- New releases in their first 30 days are for Plus Shine and Plus Blaze.
- What you can rent: PS5 and Switch games, books, films on Blu-ray and, where offered, board games. Consoles are rented only as game night kits.

## 5. Looking after rented items

- Rented items stay our property. Use them for personal use only; don't copy, resell, lend out or modify them.
- Return them in the condition you received them, apart from normal wear.
- **Damage and loss:** you are responsible for items while you have them. If one is lost, stolen or damaged beyond normal wear, we may charge a fair repair or replacement cost, which we explain before charging.

## 6. Keep it

Love something you are renting? Buy that copy at its used price, minus one month's fee of your plan, from [Your Plus](/plus#yours). It is then yours and leaves your rental slots.

## 7. Changing, pausing and cancelling

- **Change plan** in Your Plus. A move to a plan with fewer items takes effect once you have no more items at home than the new plan allows.
- **Pause** for up to one month a year. While paused you aren't charged and no new items are sent; items at home stay with you.
- **Cancel any time** in Your Plus. The cancellation takes effect at the end of the month you paid for, once your items are back. We don't refund part of a month.
- **Unreturned items:** if an item isn't back within 14 days after your plan ends, or within 14 days of our reminder, and you don't reply, we may charge its replacement cost.
