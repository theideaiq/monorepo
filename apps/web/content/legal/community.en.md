**Building a safer digital Iraq, together.**

The IDEA is a community of young people from across Iraq and all walks of life, brought together by a commitment to tolerance, to breaking stereotypes and to building partnerships. To keep a thriving community where we support, listen to and learn from one another, we ask everyone to follow these guidelines.

They apply in The Club's rooms on Telegram (https://t.me/theideaclub), in conversations with Droid, in comments and in everything you submit to The IDEA, including Journal submissions. Our [Code of conduct](/legal/conduct) and [Terms of service](/legal/terms) also apply.

## 1. The golden rules

- **Treat others with respect.** Treat others as you would like to be treated. We do not tolerate harassment, bullying or toxicity.
- **Keep it civil.** Debate is welcome, but criticize ideas, not people. Avoid name-calling and personal attacks.
- **Arabic and English are both welcome.** Write in the language you're comfortable with, and be patient with members who write in the other one. Some rooms may set their own language.
- **Add value.** Make sure your message adds something. Avoid spam, walls of text, excessive capitals and off-topic posts; use the right room for each subject.

## 2. Prohibited content and behaviour

### Hate speech and discrimination

We have zero tolerance for attacks based on ethnicity, nationality, religion, sect, disability, illness, age or gender. Disrespect towards other cultures and communities is not allowed.

### Harassment and doxing

- **No harassment:** do not abuse, impersonate or intimidate others, in the rooms or in private messages.
- **No doxing:** publishing anyone's personal information (phone number, address, photos, documents) is strictly forbidden.

### Inappropriate content

- **No sexual content:** sexually explicit or suggestive media is prohibited, including in profile pictures.
- **Protecting minors:** we have zero tolerance for any harmful content involving minors and cooperate with the authorities about predatory behaviour.
- **Sensitive topics:** do not share content that glorifies self-harm, suicide, gore or cruelty to animals.

### Illegal acts and spam

- **No illegal content:** promoting terrorism, violence, illegal drugs, or sharing pirated games, books or movies is banned.
- **No spam:** do not flood the rooms or message members for self-promotion or commercial purposes without permission from our team.
- **No scams:** never ask members for money, passwords or verification codes. The IDEA's team will never ask you for your password.

## 3. Moderation and enforcement

Our moderators and Droid, our bot, keep the rooms safe. We use notices, warnings and bans:

- **Notice:** a recorded reminder for minor issues, such as light spam. Notices expire after 60 days.
- **Warning:** for repeated or more serious behaviour. Five active warnings lead to a ban.
- **Immediate ban:** for severe violations such as hate speech, gore, doxing, scams or raids.

Bans apply across all the rooms Droid moderates. Messages that break these rules may be deleted without notice. Please respect the moderators' decisions. If you believe you were treated unfairly, send an unban or support request to our team through Droid (@TheIdeaDroidBot) or email [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com); a member of staff will review it.

## 4. Journal submissions and shared work

- Submit only work that is your own. Credit others when you share their work.
- Editors review submissions blind, without seeing the author's name. Do not put your name inside the work itself, and do not contact editors to influence a decision.
- Respect the creators in our community: do not copy or republish members' work without their permission.

## 5. IDEA Plus and the store

- **Rental respect:** treat rented games, books and movies as if they were your own. Returning them damaged or dirty hurts the next member and may lead to charges.
- **Honest reviews and questions:** when you talk about products, be honest and don't post fake reviews or misleading claims.

## 6. Offline interactions

The IDEA goes beyond the screen. When you meet our delivery team or attend our events, the same rules apply. Abuse towards delivery staff or event staff is a serious offence.

By staying in our community, you agree to follow these guidelines. They are a guide, not an exhaustive list: our team may act in situations they don't describe when needed to protect the community.
