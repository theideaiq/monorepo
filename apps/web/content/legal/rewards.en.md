This policy covers Bulbs, the points of The IDEA Pass; inviting friends; and the affiliate programme.

## 1. Bulbs

- **Value:** 1 Bulb is worth 100 IQD when you spend it with The IDEA.
- **No cash value:** Bulbs can't be exchanged for money, sold or moved to another Pass.
- **Where to see them:** Pass → Bulbs shows your balance and history.

## 2. Earning Bulbs

**On purchases**, Bulbs are a share of what you pay in money for the items (not delivery):

| Who | Bulbs back |
| --- | --- |
| Every Pass holder | 3% |
| Plus Glow | 4% |
| Plus Shine | 5% |
| Plus Blaze | 6% |

Consoles, laptops and any item over 500,000 IQD earn half the rate. The IDEA Plus payments earn Bulbs at your plan's rate. Because at most 20% of an order is given away in discounts and rewards combined, an order with a large discount may earn fewer Bulbs; the checkout shows how many it will earn.

**Bonus Bulbs:**

| For | Bulbs |
| --- | --- |
| Your first order | 20 |
| Your birthday (set once in Pass → Settings) | 25 |
| A published review with photos of something you bought or rented | 5, once per product |
| Checking in at a Club event | 10, once per event |
| Linking Telegram to your Pass | 10, the first time |
| A friend you invited, once their first order is delivered | 50, up to 20 friends a month |
| A completed Sell to us request | 10 |
| Answering a rewarded survey, where offered | 2–10, by length, paid by the survey's creator |

When you take payment for Sell to us as store credit, it is paid in Bulbs worth 120% of the offer (see [Sell to us](/legal/sell)). Reviews that earned photo Bulbs are labelled as such.

## 3. When Bulbs unlock

Bulbs from an order unlock **14 days after delivery**, once the return window has closed (see [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns)). Until then they show as pending. If the order is cancelled, returned or refunded, the Bulbs it earned are cancelled.

## 4. Spending Bulbs

At checkout you can pay for all or part of an order with unlocked Bulbs, delivery included. An order paid in full with Bulbs needs no other payment. If the order is cancelled or refunded, the Bulbs you spent come back. Bulbs can't pay for The IDEA Plus at the moment.

## 5. Expiry

Bulbs expire after **12 months without activity** on your Pass (earning or spending Bulbs). We remind you after 11 months. Bulbs also end when you delete your Pass.

## 6. Invite friends

- Share your link (theideaiq.com/r/your-code) from Pass → Bulbs. A friend who is new to The IDEA (no paid or confirmed order yet) gets **10% off their first order**, up to 15,000 IQD.
- You earn **50 Bulbs** when their first order is delivered; they unlock with the order's return window and are cancelled if it is refunded. Up to 20 friends a month.
- Each person has one inviter, and you can't invite yourself. No reward is paid when the friend's order shares a phone number or address with your own orders.

## 7. Affiliates

The affiliate programme is for people aged 18 and over. Apply in Pass → Bulbs; we approve affiliates and give them a code.

- **Followers' discount:** your link or code gives the people who use it 5% off for 30 days. The last link or code used counts, and a code typed at checkout replaces a link. One discount per order: the larger of an invite and an affiliate discount.
- **Commission:** a share of the price after the discount, at the rate shown for the product's category in your affiliate page (set by us per category). It is earned when the order is delivered and becomes payable once its 14-day return window has passed. When someone you referred joins The IDEA Plus, you earn 20% of their first 3 monthly payments, or a quarter of a yearly one.
- **No commission** on your own orders or on orders that share a phone number or address with yours.
- **Refunds** cancel unpaid commission; commission already paid is taken from your next payout.
- **Payouts:** once your ready commission reaches 25,000 IQD, we pay it by ZainCash, FIB, Qi or bank transfer, or as Bulbs worth 20% more, credited at once.

**Say when you're promoting.** Whenever you share your link or code (posts, stories, videos, messages), say clearly that it is an affiliate link and that you earn from it, for example with "#ad" or "إعلان". Don't make claims we haven't made about products or prices, don't pretend to be The IDEA or our staff, don't spam people or groups, and don't use your code on coupon sites or in paid ads with our name without our agreement. We may pause or close an affiliate who breaks these rules, and withhold commission earned by breaking them.

## 8. Fair use and changes

We may cancel Bulbs, discounts or commission obtained by fraud, by several Passes for one person, or by breaking these rules, and suspend the Pass concerned (see [Terms](/legal/terms)). We may change how Bulbs are earned, the bonuses and the affiliate rates; we will tell you in advance, and Bulbs you have already earned keep their value.
