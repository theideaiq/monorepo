We show them only to identify the products we offer. If you own such material and want it used differently, contact us as in section 4.

## 3. What members share

Reviews, photos, Club submissions, survey questions and other content you share stay yours. By sharing them, you:

- confirm that they are your own work, or that you have the right to share them, and that they don't infringe anyone's rights;
- give The IDEA a non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to show, adapt for layout and translate them on our website, apps and channels, with your name or byline where shown, for as long as they are published.

You can ask us to remove your content at any time; we will take it off the service, though copies already shared by others may remain. Content that breaks the [Club rules](/legal/club) may be removed without notice.

## 4. Takedown requests

If you believe material on our services infringes your copyright or trademark, email [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com) with "Copyright" in the subject and include:

- your name and contact details, and, if you act for someone else, whom you represent;
- the work you own, and where it appears on our services (links or screenshots);
- a statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorised, and that the information in your request is accurate and that you own the rights or may act for the owner;
- your signature (a typed name is enough).

We review every complete request, remove or disable the material where appropriate and tell the person who shared it. If they believe it was removed by mistake, they can reply with their reasons, and we may restore it unless the rights holder takes the matter further. We may share a request with the person who posted the material. Members who repeatedly infringe others' rights lose access to the service.

## 5. Rented and purchased items

Buying or renting a game, album, film or book doesn't give you the right to copy, share or broadcast it. Copying rented items is forbidden (see [The IDEA Plus](/legal/plus)), and pirated material is not allowed in The IDEA Club.
