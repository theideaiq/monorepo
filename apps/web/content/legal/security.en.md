We welcome reports from anyone who finds a security weakness in The IDEA's services. This policy explains how to report one and what you can expect from us. It is also published in machine-readable form at [/.well-known/security.txt](https://theideaiq.com/.well-known/security.txt).

## 1. Scope

- theideaiq.com, including The IDEA Pass and the cart and checkout;
- partners.theideaiq.com (The IDEA Partners), staff.theideaiq.com (Staff and The IDEA Design) and api.theideaiq.com (the API and developer docs);
- The IDEA's iOS and Android apps;
- The IDEA Droid on Telegram.

Services run by others (such as Wayl, Boxy, Telegram or our hosting providers) have their own security programmes; report weaknesses in them to those companies.

## 2. How to report

Email [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com) with "Security" in the subject. Please include:

- what you found, and where (the address, screen or feature);
- the steps to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it;
- your name or handle, if you would like to be credited.

Write in English or Arabic. Please don't report weaknesses publicly, in the Club or on social media, until we have fixed them.

## 3. Rules for testing

- Use only your own The IDEA Pass, or test ones you create. Never access, change or delete other people's data; if you reach it by accident, stop, don't keep it, and tell us.
- Don't disrupt the service: no denial-of-service attacks, spam, or heavy automated scanning.
- Don't use social engineering or phishing on our staff, riders or members, and don't test our store or offices physically.
- Don't place real orders you don't mean to pay for, or try to obtain goods, Bulbs or payments.

## 4. What we do

- We confirm that we received your report, keep you informed and tell you when it is fixed.
- We won't take legal action against anyone who reports in good faith and follows this policy.
- With your permission, we thank you by name once the issue is fixed. We don't run a paid bounty programme.

## 5. Keeping your Pass safe

The IDEA will never ask for your sign-in code, by phone, email, Telegram or WhatsApp. Sign-in codes expire after a few minutes. If you get a message claiming to be from The IDEA that asks for one, don't reply, and tell us.
