Every copy we sell has one of four condition labels, shown on the product page before you buy. This policy explains what each one means, how we check items, the warranty that comes with each, and how we show market prices.

## 1. The four conditions

| Condition | What it means | Typical price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **New** | Unused and in its original packaging, sealed where the maker seals it. | The full price |
| **Open box** | The packaging has been opened (for example a display or returned item), but the item is unused or as good as unused and complete. | At most 75% of new |
| **Refurbished** | Used before, then checked, cleaned and repaired where needed by us or the maker, and working fully. | About 80–85% of new |
| **Used** | Owned before, with the signs of use described on the product page. Works as it should. | About 50–65% of new, by wear |

When a copy isn't new, its product page describes anything you should know, such as wear on a sleeve, a missing manual or a mark on a case. Photos of collector pieces show the actual item where we can.

## 2. How we check items

- **Every copy** is checked when it comes in: that it is genuine, complete and matches its listing.
- **Open box, Refurbished and Used** items are also tested: discs and records for playback and scratches, devices for power, controls and connections, books for missing or damaged pages.
- We don't sell counterfeit or pirated games, records, films or electronics.
- **Vintage hi-fi and cameras** are one-off pieces; you can view them by appointment before buying.

## 3. Warranty

| Condition | Warranty |
| --- | --- |
| New | The maker's warranty, where one exists, as shown on the product page. |
| Open box | The maker's warranty where it still applies; otherwise the warranty shown on the product page. |
| Refurbished | Our own warranty, for the period shown on the product page. |
| Used | No warranty beyond the 14-day return window, unless the product page says otherwise. |

Iraq's Consumer Protection Law requires a warranty for goods that need one; where it does, the product page states it. Every order comes with a receipt showing the price, date and specifications of what you bought; keep it for warranty claims.

**To make a warranty claim,** contact us with your order number and a description or video of the fault. We will repair, replace or refund the item, or help you claim from the maker. A warranty doesn't cover damage from accidents, misuse, liquid or unauthorised repairs.

Within 14 days of delivery, every condition can be returned when faulty, and Open box, Refurbished and Used items when they are not as described (see [Returns and refunds](/legal/returns)).

## 4. Market ("souq") prices

Some product pages compare our price with the price in Iraq's market (the souq). We show a market price only when we have checked it in the last 7 days and saved the evidence (where and when it was seen). If we haven't, we don't show one.

## 5. Age ratings

Games show their PEGI age rating (3, 7, 12, 16 or 18). Games rated PEGI 18 and other mature items are hidden from and not sold to members under 18.
