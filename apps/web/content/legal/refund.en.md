Thank you for choosing The IDEA. This policy explains how cancellations, returns and refunds work for Megastore orders, IDEA Plus memberships and rentals, IDEA Suite business plans and Academy programs. It does not limit any rights you have under Iraqi consumer protection law.

## 1. Megastore orders

### Cancelling an order

- **Before payment:** an order awaiting payment can be cancelled from your orders in The IDEA app at any time.
- **After payment, before shipping:** contact us as soon as possible at [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com) with your order number. If the order has not been handed to our delivery team yet, we will cancel it and refund you in full.
- **After shipping:** you can refuse the delivery or return the item as described below.

### 30-day returns

You can ask to return an item within **30 days of receiving it**. To be eligible, it must be unused, in the condition you received it, with its tags, accessories and original packaging, and with proof of purchase (your order in the app).

To start a return, contact us at [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com) with your order number and the reason. We will tell you how and where to send or hand over the item. Items sent back without first asking us will not be accepted.

### Items that cannot be returned

Unless they are faulty, we cannot accept returns of:

- opened software, games and other digital codes or downloads;
- personal-care and hygiene products once opened;
- personalized or custom-made items;
- gift cards;
- items marked as final sale on the product page.

### Faulty, damaged or wrong items

If an item arrives damaged, faulty or is not what you ordered, tell us within **7 days of delivery** with photos if possible. We will replace it or refund you in full, including any delivery fee, and arrange the pickup at no cost to you. Products with a manufacturer's warranty are covered for the warranty period shown on the product page.

### Refunds

Once we receive and inspect a returned item, we will tell you whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are sent back to the original payment method through our payment partner **Wayl**, usually within 10 business days; your bank or wallet may take longer to show it. Delivery fees are refunded only when the return is our fault (a faulty, damaged or wrong item). Used or refurbished items may be returned under the same conditions, judged against their condition as described when you bought them.

## 2. IDEA Plus membership

- **Cancel any time:** you can cancel your membership from your account in the app.
- **Billing period:** after you cancel, your membership stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and you will not be charged again. We do not refund partial months.
- **Faulty charges:** if you were charged by mistake, or twice, contact us and we will refund the extra charge.

## 3. IDEA Plus rentals

- **Before delivery:** a rental request can be cancelled in the app until the item is handed to our delivery team, and it frees your rental slot immediately.
- **Returning items:** return rented items when you are done or when your membership ends, in the condition you received them (normal wear excepted). Rentals are part of your membership, so there is no separate refund for returning an item early.
- **Faulty items:** if a rented disc, book or movie is faulty or damaged when it reaches you, tell us within 3 days and we will replace it at no cost.
- **Loss or damage:** charges for lost or damaged items are explained in our [Terms of service](/legal/terms).

## 4. IDEA Suite business plans

Business plans renew each billing period until you cancel. You can cancel from your organization's settings in the app; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period and partial periods are not refunded. Procurement orders placed through IDEA Suite follow the Megastore rules above unless your agreement with us says otherwise.

## 5. Academy programs

Academy programs and exchanges may have their own agreement, which sets out their fees and refund terms. Unless that agreement says otherwise:

- application fees are non-refundable;
- program fees are refunded in part depending on how early you cancel, as stated in your program agreement;
- if we cancel a program, you receive a full refund.

## 6. Events

Most of our events are free. For paid events, the refund terms are shown when you register; if we cancel an event, you receive a full refund.

Returns, cancellations and refunds: [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com). Please include your order number.
