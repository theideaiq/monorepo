The IDEA Club is the free side of The IDEA: on [/club](/club), Join (events and surveys), Read (posts) and Yours, and our chats on Telegram. Everyone with The IDEA Pass is in it. These rules keep it a place where everyone, whatever their age, background or opinions, can take part safely.

## 1. Where these rules apply

- the Club feed on the website and apps, and everything you submit to it;
- reviews and photos on product pages;
- The IDEA Club group on Telegram and its rooms (The Stage, The Lounge, The Arcade, Vibe Check, The Library, The Studio and The Office), our Telegram channel and conversations with The IDEA Droid;
- our events, the counter and every meeting with our staff and riders;
- everyone who takes part: members, guests, staff, partners and speakers.

Surveys also follow [Surveys](/legal/surveys), and The IDEA's programs follow [Programs](/legal/programs).

## 2. Joining

Anyone can read the feed. RSVPs, submissions and surveys need The IDEA Pass. To join The IDEA Club group on Telegram, link your Telegram to your Pass in Pass → Settings; The IDEA Droid then approves your request to join.

## 3. Expected behaviour

- **Respect people.** Treat others as you want to be treated. Criticise ideas, not people.
- **Be welcoming.** Arabic and English are both welcome; be patient with members who write in the other language.
- **Stay on topic.** Use the right room, and add something to the conversation.
- **Respect boundaries.** Stop when someone asks you to, online and in person.

## 4. Not allowed

- **Hate and discrimination:** attacks on anyone for their ethnicity, nationality, religion, sect, disability, illness, age or gender.
- **Harassment:** bullying, threats, intimidation, stalking, impersonation or unwanted contact, in public or private messages.
- **Doxing:** sharing anyone's personal information (phone number, address, photos, documents) without permission.
- **Sexual content:** sexual or suggestive material, including in profile pictures, and unwanted sexual attention.
- **Harm to minors:** any harmful content involving minors, and any adult seeking private contact with a member under 18. We report this to the authorities.
- **Violence and danger:** glorifying violence, terrorism, self-harm, suicide, gore or cruelty to animals; promoting illegal drugs.
- **Piracy and illegal content:** sharing pirated games, music, books or films, or copies of rented items.
- **Spam and scams:** advertising or self-promotion without our permission, chain messages, asking members for money or sign-in codes. The IDEA's staff will never ask for your sign-in code.
- **Fake reviews:** reviews must be honest and about something you bought or rented.

## 5. Events

- RSVP with your Pass; your card is checked at the door. When places are limited, Plus Blaze members can book in the first 48 hours. An RSVP doesn't guarantee a place once an event is full.
- **Under-16s** come with an adult. Members under 18 follow the age rules in our [Terms](/legal/terms).
- **No alcohol** is served at The IDEA's events. Using or promoting illegal drugs leads to immediate removal.
- **Photos:** we may take photos and videos at events for the Club and our channels. Tell the organisers if you don't want to be photographed, and ask us to remove a photo of you at any time. Don't photograph other people without their permission, and never share photos of minors without their guardian's agreement.
- **Access:** tell the organisers in advance if you need step-free access or other help, and see [Accessibility](/legal/accessibility).

## 6. Submissions

Submit only your own work, and credit others when you share theirs. Editors review submissions without seeing the author's name, and their decisions are final. Don't put your name in the work or contact editors to influence a decision. Published work keeps your byline; see [Copyright](/legal/copyright) for the licence you give us.

## 7. Reporting

If you are harassed, see something that breaks these rules, or feel unsafe:

- in Telegram, use Telegram's report button and message The IDEA Droid → Talk to us;
- at an event, speak to any member of staff;
- by email, [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com).

Reports are taken seriously and handled in confidence. In an emergency, call the police or emergency services first.

## 8. Moderation

Our moderators use notices, warnings and bans:

- **Notice:** a reminder for small things, such as light spam. Notices expire after 60 days.
- **Warning:** for repeated or more serious behaviour. Five active warnings lead to a ban.
- **Immediate ban:** for severe violations such as hate speech, harm to minors, gore, doxing, scams or raids.

We may delete content that breaks these rules without notice, remove someone from an event without a refund, suspend their Pass and, where the law requires, report them. Bans apply across all of The IDEA Club. Aggression towards our staff or riders, in chat, by phone or at your door, leads to a permanent ban.

**Appeals:** if you think a decision was unfair, write to us through The IDEA Droid or by email; a member of staff who wasn't involved will review it.

These rules are a guide, not a complete list: we may act in situations they don't describe to protect the Club.
