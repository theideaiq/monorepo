The IDEA should be easy for everyone to use, including people with disabilities. This page explains what we do and how to tell us about a barrier.

## 1. Our commitment

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA on theideaiq.com, The IDEA Business and our iOS and Android apps.

## 2. What we do

- Every page works in Arabic (right to left) and English (left to right).
- Pages can be used with a keyboard and with screen readers, have clear headings and labels, and keep working when you zoom text to 200%.
- Colours are chosen for contrast, in light and dark themes, and colour is never the only way information is shown.
- We test our main journeys (signing in, the store, the cart and checkout, The IDEA Pass) with automated accessibility checks before each release.

## 3. Known limitations

- Some product photos and older Club posts may lack full text descriptions.
- Pages run by others, such as Wayl's payment page and Telegram, follow their own accessibility.

We are working on these. If one stops you from doing something, contact us and we will help you another way, for example by taking your order through The IDEA Droid or by email.

## 4. At events and the store

Tell the organisers in advance if you need step-free access, a seat, a companion or help following the language of an event, and we will plan for it. See [Club rules](/legal/club).

## 5. Report a barrier

Write to [hi@theideaiq.com](mailto:hi@theideaiq.com) or use [Help](/help#contact). Tell us the page or screen, what you were trying to do, and the device, browser or assistive technology you use. We will reply, fix what we can and tell you what we did.
